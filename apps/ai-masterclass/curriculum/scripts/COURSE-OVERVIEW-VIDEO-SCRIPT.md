# AI Master Class Course Overview Script

## Status

Draft outline only. Final spoken wording waits for approved sources and a locked module sequence.

## Spoken intent

Welcome the learner, explain that the series is about useful and accountable AI in utility work,
describe the sequence from foundational language through context, agents, guardrails, and a bounded
pilot, and explain that each lesson produces a practical work product.

## Visual direction

Show the learner pathway, the relationship between source evidence, utility context, human
accountability, Articulate learning, and the One Water knowledge graph. Do not imply that course
completion authorizes a live operating decision.
