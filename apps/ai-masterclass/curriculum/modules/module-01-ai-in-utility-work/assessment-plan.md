# Module 01 Assessment Plan

| Check | Type | What it verifies | Feedback requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Responsibility sorting | classification | learner separates assistance, review, and authorization | explain why the role boundary matters |
| Context comparison | question flip cards | learner recognizes why source boundaries matter | explain the evidence or context consequence |
| Handoff sequence | ordering | learner can place review and authorization in the right order | show a numbered corrected sequence |
| Boundary decision | applied scenario | learner can select a safeguard for a bounded utility use case | connect rationale to the boundary card |

Completion cannot rely on scrolling. The learner must complete required interactions, answer the
final applied check, and save or complete the boundary card.
