## The decision

One Water AI will compete for the attention of professionals who could otherwise select an applied
artificial intelligence, executive education, agentic artificial intelligence, or technical
engineering program from a major university. It will do that by offering a combination those
programs do not visibly bring together in one curriculum: artificial intelligence depth, water
practice, One Water relationships, production discipline, governance, provenance, human authority,
professional work products, and a defended organizational pilot.

The existing 64-module core remains intact. It is the common language every participant shares.
Technical preparation and advanced theory become optional extension lines with their own entry
requirements, workload, outcomes, and evidence. This prevents the executive, operator, clerk,
finance, policy, and procurement audiences from being forced through a generic programming boot
camp while giving builders and engineers a serious technical route.

## Learning-objective standard for every accepted expansion

Every affected module will add objectives that require the learner to:

1. explain the new subject in plain English;
2. distinguish the terms that are commonly collapsed;
3. analyze a water, wastewater, stormwater, or One Water situation;
4. identify sources, permissions, limitations, failure, and human authority;
5. choose and defend a bounded approach;
6. create a professional work product; and
7. respond to a failure, disagreement, or correction with an accountable next action.

Objectives must map to teaching, practice, feedback, assessment, work-product evidence, and a
completion event. An attractive description does not count as an objective.

## Milestone projects

The core and extensions will show four named milestones:

1. **Governed Research Assistant:** retrieve and synthesize approved utility sources, show exact
   evidence, mark conflicts, refuse unsupported claims, and preserve a research record.
2. **Read-Only Utility Workflow Agent:** use one approved tool, identity, and permission boundary to
   prepare work without operating a physical system or changing an authoritative record.
3. **Multi-Agent Decision Room:** coordinate specialist perspectives, shared evidence, disagreement,
   synthesis, failure, and a qualified human decision.
4. **Governed 90-Day Pilot:** defend the problem, baseline, data, architecture, controls, tests,
   economics, workforce plan, stop conditions, and next accountable action.

Every project must show what failed, what changed, who reviewed it, and what evidence survives.

- the evidence required before any certificate, continuing education unit, or university-equivalence
  claim can be made.

## Competitive-positioning rule

Allowed internal positioning:

> One Water AI is designed as a full applied artificial intelligence curriculum for drinking water,
> wastewater, stormwater, and cross-functional One Water work. It combines technical fluency,
> governed agent systems, utility applications, production reliability, data and knowledge
> foundations, human authority, procurement, workforce adoption, professional work products, and a
> defended pilot in one connected program.

Prohibited without a separately verified market study:

- the only program in the world;
- better than Harvard, Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Johns Hopkins University, or Virginia
  Tech;
- equivalent to a university degree, certificate, continuing education unit, or accreditation;
- guaranteed career, salary, utility, compliance, safety, or performance outcomes; and
- finished, production-ready, or released before the required artifacts and gates exist.
