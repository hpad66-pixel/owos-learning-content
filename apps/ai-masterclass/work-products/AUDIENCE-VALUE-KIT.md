# One Water AI Audience Value Kit

## Principle

Free material should prove the quality of the thinking and help someone make one better decision.
It should not give away the complete fellowship, capstone, expert review, or participant community.

## Public lead resources

### 1. The One Water AI Terms Field Card

A concise, visual guide to model, chatbot, retrieval, workflow, agent, agentic system, skill,
tool, context, knowledge graph, evaluation, guardrail, and human owner.

**Format:** two-page PDF and mobile web page.

**Purpose:** give a utility professional enough language to participate in the first conversation.

### 2. The 15-Minute Utility AI Readiness Check

A short self-assessment covering problem clarity, data, knowledge, ownership, privacy, security,
review, measurement, and workforce readiness.

**Format:** interactive scorecard with a downloadable result.

**Purpose:** help a prospective participant see what the fellowship will help them resolve.

### 3. Ten Utility AI Opportunities and the Questions to Ask First

Ten bounded examples across operations, maintenance, water quality, customer service, capital,
stormwater, wastewater, drinking water, finance, and administration. Every example includes the
value, required sources, human owner, red flag, and simplest alternative.

**Format:** illustrated briefing PDF.

**Purpose:** demonstrate utility specificity without presenting an implementation recipe.

### 4. Before You Buy an AI Solution

A one-page procurement conversation guide covering the problem, sources, data use, permissions,
evaluation, incidents, exit terms, and evidence.

**Format:** printable checklist.

**Purpose:** provide immediate practical value to utility buyers.

### 5. A public Module 1 preview

A 20-minute Articulate lesson containing the opening decision, central distinction, one graphic,
one interaction, one knowledge check, and a sample boundary card.

**Purpose:** let prospects experience the teaching quality before applying.

### 6. One Water AI Office Hours

A monthly public session where one utility problem is reframed without exposing confidential data.

**Purpose:** demonstrate the faculty's judgment and build trust over time.

## Included participant resources

The following are included with tuition and should not be distributed as ordinary lead magnets:

- the complete One Water AI Fieldbook;
- all reusable templates and completed examples;
- the Claude Skill Builder Pack;
- the Utility Agent Canvas;
- the AI procurement scorecard;
- the governance and control record;
- the failure and evaluation laboratory;
- the capstone review rubric;
- live forums and expert studios;
- instructor and peer feedback;
- the private cohort community;
- the capstone defense; and
- continued access defined by the enrollment agreement.

## Employer sponsorship kit

Provide applicants with:

- a one-page program brief;
- a manager-approval letter template;
- a workload and calendar summary;
- a statement of organizational deliverables;
- a tuition and cancellation sheet;
- a confidentiality and data-handling explanation;
- a sample final portfolio table of contents; and
- a simple return-on-learning worksheet.

This makes the $10,000 request easier to evaluate because the employer can see the time, outputs,
boundaries, and organizational value before approving tuition.
