## First course: provisional sequence

1. AI and utility work: what changes, what does not, and who remains accountable
2. The language of AI: model, prompt, data, automation, assistant, agent, and agentic system
3. From question to answer: context, retrieval, and limits
4. Anatomy of an AI agent: goal, tools, memory, handoff, and guardrails
5. Safe utility use cases and red flags
6. Data, governance, privacy, and security before scale
7. Designing a bounded pilot with a human accountable owner
8. Presenting an AI use case and a 90-day learning plan

The sequence above is a starting frame. Research and Hardeep's instruction can add, merge, or
split modules before blueprint approval.
