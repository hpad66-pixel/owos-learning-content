Value first

Every module earns its place.

The manuscript names who receives value, what professional action improves, and what the learner can carry back to work.

Citation discipline

A direct locator travels with each claim.

A useful citation identifies the original issuer, date, direct URL, and exact page, section, table, figure, or paragraph.

Graphic discipline

The manuscript explains the picture before it is drawn.

For every graphic, it states the teaching job, natural shape, reading order, learner action, accessible meaning, mobile form, and conclusion.

Stop condition

Unsupported claims do not move forward.

If the original source cannot be opened, located, or applied honestly, the claim stays qualified, unresolved, or removed.

Before every research run

Give Perplexity a professional question, not a topic.

Broad searches create broad summaries. A controlled research brief produces claim leads, direct sources, applicability limits, and unresolved questions that a fact checker can examine.

01

State the utility decision

What must a utility professional understand, compare, approve, or produce after this module?

02

Request original sources

Prioritize United States authorities, standards issuers, peer-reviewed research, and first-party technical documentation.

03

Demand exact locators

Ask for the page, section, table, figure, or paragraph that supports each proposed claim.

04

Ask what remains uncertain

Record conflicts, limitations, time sensitivity, geographic boundaries, and claims that still need verification.

Research [MODULE TITLE] for water, wastewater, and stormwater professionals in the United States. Begin with this professional question: [QUESTION]. The learner must produce [WORK PRODUCT]. For every factual claim, provide the original source, direct URL, publication date, exact locator, what the source supports, its limitation, its utility applicability, and any credible disagreement. Separate sourced fact, expert interpretation, Hardeep Anand's position, instructional scenario, and unresolved question. Do not invent quotations, statistics, examples, or citations. End with a source table and a list of claims that still require verification.
