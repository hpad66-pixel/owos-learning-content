# M00 curriculum scrub and content movement direction

Date: 2026-08-06
Owner: Hardeep Anand
Destination: `apps/ai-masterclass/`
Status: approved for prototype implementation

## Direction

Hardeep directed the Academy team to scrub the content under every module for curriculum fit and
learner sense. Existing material must not be deleted. When material belongs elsewhere, the Academy
must support a governed move, copy, cross-reference, or optional-placement proposal that preserves
the source, contributor, prior location, reason, reviewer, and revision history.

The first prototype is legacy Module M00, `Orientation, Setup & Your Learning Path`. The current
proposal inventory places terminal use, application programming interface keys, secret files, and
code-editor setup inside M00. The prototype must preserve those proposals while deciding whether
they are required orientation, optional preparation, or better placed in later technical and
governance modules.

For M00, staff must receive direct, plain-English instructions. The module must identify observable
learner outcomes, curriculum outcomes, practical outcomes, internal marketing outcomes, required
evidence, activities, assessment, work product, scope boundaries, graphics, production direction,
and quality-control criteria. Staff should not have to infer the assignment.

The prototype must also include a reusable artificial-intelligence research and production prompt.
That prompt must require a goal and plan before drafting, distinguish evidence from interpretation,
use the One Water context, preserve provenance, and keep proposed content outside approved
learner-facing instruction until review.

## Approval boundary

This direction approves the M00 blueprint and Academy workflow prototype. It does not approve the
learner-facing M00 lesson, the movement of canonical curriculum content, public marketing claims,
Articulate production, credentialing, or course release.
