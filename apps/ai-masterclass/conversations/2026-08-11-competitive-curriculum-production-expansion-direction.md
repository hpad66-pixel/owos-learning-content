# Competitive Curriculum Production Expansion Direction

## Date

2026-08-11

## Direction from Hardeep Anand

Hardeep approved integrating the findings from the applied and agentic artificial intelligence
competitive audit into the curriculum. He authorized new modules, lessons, content, descriptions,
learning objectives, and program explanations where they add value. He directed the curriculum to
be developed at a level that can compete for professional attention with major university programs,
including Virginia Tech, Harvard University, Massachusetts Institute of Technology, and Johns
Hopkins University.

Hardeep directed the team to create a detailed plan first and then implement it. The implementation
must make the curriculum easier to understand and prepare it for production.

## Interpretation and boundary

This direction approves curriculum-blueprint implementation of the audit recommendations. It does
not turn third-party brochures into water-sector authority, approve copied course language, prove
that no comparable offering exists anywhere, authorize a university-equivalence claim, approve a
credential or continuing education unit, or release unfinished learner-facing lessons.

The implementation keeps the universal 64-module Fellowship core and adds optional technical and
advanced pathways so different professional audiences share one foundation without receiving the
same prerequisite burden.
