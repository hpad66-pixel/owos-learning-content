# Direction: show staff how every module gets built

Date: 2026-08-03

Hardeep directed the course team to show the complete module-production structure on a landing page
in a clear HTML diagram. Every module begins with research from multiple sources. Perplexity is used
to support cited research discovery. The original sources must be preserved and fact-checked. The
result becomes a complete Markdown paper with citations, plain-English teaching, and a specific
description of the graphics that should accentuate, amplify, or exemplify each important point.

The module value proposition must remain visible. Staff must understand the process before beginning
research. The workflow therefore needs a reusable research instruction, a controlled Markdown
template, saved source and claim records, graphic directions, Articulate handoff, and visible QA and
approval gates.
