# Role Tracks, Research Studio, and Growth System Direction

## Date

2026-08-05

## Direction from Hardeep Anand

Hardeep asked for a thorough end-to-end test of the Academy and for the next layer of value needed
to make it a state-of-the-art course and operating ecosystem.

He directed the team to create learning tracks for administrators, clerks, finance executives,
budget professionals, C-suite leaders, elected officials, vendors, consultants, engineers,
planners, operators, builders and product creators, and chief information, security, and AI
officers. The tracks must help each learner understand water, wastewater, stormwater, data
governance, artificial intelligence, operations, planning, engineering, products, challenges, and
the people and professions around them. Learners should be able to grow their professional profile
in both the water sector and the technology and AI sector.

Hardeep asked for every module to be expanded as a valuable first pass for the research team. Each
module should explain what is being built and why, use examples and analogies, connect the relevant
roles and disciplines, give diagram direction, and include a strong prompt that can guide the
research for that module and lesson.

He also directed the Academy application to support Markdown creation, a beautiful right-side
preview, in-application editing and review, saved revisions, GitHub handoff, graphics and asset
files, inspiration, sketches, annotations, and contributor-level change tracking.

For go-to-market work, Hardeep asked for track-specific landing-page concepts, HubSpot and
GoHighLevel integration options, and a mock analytics system showing how real-time performance,
notes, spend, customer acquisition cost, and related evidence will eventually be connected.

Finally, Hardeep directed the work to begin with a detailed plan file, implement the plan fully,
test the complete system, and publish the private Academy application to the agreed Academy site
and custom subdomain path.

## Implemented interpretation

- The request for 34 modules is treated as a minimum scope. The governed Fellowship contains 64
  modules, so every one of the 64 received a research starter.
- The phrase "CIA officers" is interpreted as chief information officers, chief information
  security officers, and chief AI officers. It is not interpreted as an intelligence-agency track.
- Research starters are blueprints, not completed lessons. Each remains subject to source,
  evidence, practitioner, novice, accessibility, design, production, and release review.
- Landing-page concepts and analytics samples are internal planning material. They are not public
  claims or actual performance reporting.
- Application revisions and Git commits remain separate. The Academy can record a reviewed Git
  handoff, but it must not claim repository synchronization until a real commit exists.

## Authority and boundary

This direction authorizes curriculum architecture, research-starter generation, role-track design,
internal application development, testing, and private deployment. It does not authorize public
course release, credential claims, verified learner outcomes, live CRM data transmission, or
unreviewed Git promotion.
