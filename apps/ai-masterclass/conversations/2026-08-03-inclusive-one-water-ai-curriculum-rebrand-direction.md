# Inclusive One Water AI Curriculum Rebrand Direction

## Decision

Hardeep approved the inclusive program identity for the complete 64-module source curriculum:

- **Program name:** One Water AI
- **Subtitle:** The Applied Intelligence Curriculum for the Water Sector
- **Cover promise:** One shared foundation, seven role-based tracks, and 64 modules

The complete curriculum must not be presented as executive-only. It serves practitioners,
executives, elected officials, students, academia, vendors and industry partners, consultants,
governments, operators, planners, and engineers. The Executive Fellowship remains a premium cohort
and delivery option inside the broader One Water AI curriculum. It is not the name of the entire
curriculum library.

## Approved tracks

1. Utility Operations and Practice
2. Engineering, Planning and Capital Delivery
3. Executive and Public Leadership
4. Data, AI and Governance
5. Consulting and Industry Innovation
6. Research and Academia
7. Emerging Professionals and Students

## Build boundary

The legacy 64-module source library remains untouched. A reproducible builder applies the approved
identity to temporary working copies, generates a new cover and contents, renders the complete PDF,
and creates a self-contained interactive HTML reader. No public release is authorized by this
decision.
