## Scope interpretation

The governed registry contains two different 64-module lines. M00 is the first module in the legacy
Applied Intelligence source curriculum, which runs from M00 through M63. The remaining scope is
therefore the 63 legacy modules M01 through M63. The separate Fellowship line, M1 through M64,
already has governed research starters and remains a curated delivery sequence. It will not be
duplicated or silently treated as the source curriculum.

## Approval meaning

This direction approves M00 as the blueprint and staff-direction pattern for the remaining legacy
modules. It authorizes production of reviewable module guidance, outcomes, placement registers,
design briefs, and research prompts. It does not approve unsupported factual claims, learner-facing
lesson manuscripts, Articulate production, credential claims, Knowledge Graph publication, or
public release. Those gates remain separate.

## Implementation result

The direction produced 63 new unique specifications and complete governed guidance packages for
all 64 legacy modules. The packages preserve 1,397 granular current sections, proposals, proposal
subtopics, enhancements, and contributor records. The Academy can edit each blueprint, search and
filter each module's content inventory, review provenance, and create versioned placement proposals.
Automated contracts, the Academy build and tests, Hardeep voice checks, desktop browser review, and
phone browser review passed. Full learner-facing conformance remains not eligible until each module
has verified research, a complete manuscript, production files, reviewer evidence, and owner release.
