# Black and Gold Curriculum Design Direction

## Direction

Hardeep rejected the pale blue curriculum cover and reader shell. The complete One Water AI
curriculum must belong to the same document family as the One Water AI Fieldbook and The Steersman.
The design should feel bold, editorial, and established, using a black background, warm ivory type,
and restrained gold accents.

## Implementation decision

- Use a near-black cover with ivory title typography and a gold editorial subtitle.
- Use restrained gold rules, track borders, and concentric geometry.
- Remove blue from the curriculum cover, contents identity, and interactive reader shell.
- Use cream and charcoal for the contents and long-form reading surfaces so the 684-page document
  remains comfortable to read and practical to print.
- Preserve the complete 64-module curriculum, the seven role-based tracks, and all source material.
- Keep the source library unchanged and regenerate both final formats through the governed builder.
