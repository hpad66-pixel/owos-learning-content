# Granular TOC and Gap-Analysis Direction

Date: 2026-08-04

Hardeep Anand supplied `AI MASTER CLASS GAP ANALYSIS.docx` and directed the course team to compare
it against the complete compiled curriculum, analyze the table of contents, and first establish a
more granular, collapsible curriculum map.

The review layer must:

- preserve the current 64-module numbering;
- show current module sections and subsections;
- show proposed additions beside the modules where they belong;
- use stable identifiers so changes can be tracked at module, lesson, and subtopic level;
- preserve the source document and exact checksum;
- distinguish missing, partial, duplicate, current, and proposed content;
- keep source-page locators for the review document and current PDF page spans for the curriculum;
  and
- avoid treating a curriculum recommendation as factual authority.

The first delivery is the granular tracker and review interface. Integrating approved additions
into the complete curriculum PDF and HTML reader is a later step after curriculum decisions are
recorded.
