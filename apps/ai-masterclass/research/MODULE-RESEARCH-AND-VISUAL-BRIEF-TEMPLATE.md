## 3. Perplexity research brief

Perplexity may help discover sources and compare leads. Its answer is not the authority. Open every
material source, preserve its original URL or file, and verify the claim against the source itself.

Copy and complete this research instruction:

```text
Research the following module for the One Water AI Executive Fellowship:

Module: [TITLE]
Audience: water, wastewater, and stormwater professionals in the United States
Primary question: [QUESTION]
Intended work product: [WORK PRODUCT]

Find current, credible sources that directly support the teaching. Prioritize original and primary
sources, including United States government authorities, standards issuers, peer-reviewed research,
and first-party technical documentation. Use secondary sources only to explain or compare, never as
a substitute for the original authority.

For every proposed factual claim, return:
1. the exact claim in plain English;
2. the original source title and issuer;
3. the direct source URL;
4. the publication or update date;
5. the exact page, section, table, figure, or paragraph locator;
6. what the source actually supports;
7. the limitation, uncertainty, or transfer boundary;
8. whether the claim may change with time;
9. whether it applies to United States water, wastewater, or stormwater practice; and
10. any conflicting source or credible disagreement.

Separate sourced fact, expert interpretation, Hardeep Anand's position, instructional scenario,
and unresolved question. Do not invent quotations, statistics, operating examples, or citations.
End with a source table and a list of claims that still require verification.
```

| | | | | yes / no / qualified | | | | open / verified / rejected |

Fact-check rules:

1. Open the original source. Do not rely on the Perplexity summary.
2. Read enough surrounding context to understand what the source does and does not say.
3. Confirm the author, issuer, publication date, version, and exact locator.
4. Confirm United States and utility applicability.
5. Record contrary evidence, uncertainty, and time-sensitive limitations.
6. Rewrite or remove any statement that the evidence does not directly support.
7. Keep Hardeep's positions attributed to Hardeep.
