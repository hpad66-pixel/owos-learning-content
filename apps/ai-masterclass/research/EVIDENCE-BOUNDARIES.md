# Evidence Boundaries

## Module 01

No factual claim has been approved for public instruction yet. The current materials define the
authoring and teaching structure only.

Before production, source review must establish:

- the technical definition and scope of each AI term;
- the limits of the utility examples;
- which claims depend on current technology, law, security practice, or policy;
- which statements are Hardeep Anand's stated position; and
- which examples are instructional scenarios rather than operating advice.

No source found through YouTube, LinkedIn, Perplexity, or an AI summary becomes authoritative until
its original authority, context, date, and applicability are reviewed.
