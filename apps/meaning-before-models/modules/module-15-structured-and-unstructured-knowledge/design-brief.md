# Module 15 Design Brief: Structured and Unstructured Knowledge

Status: approved for production under standing owner authorization

## Learning decision

The learner must preserve a conflict among records and documents, distinguish retrieval from
authority, and promote a passage to reviewed knowledge only after source, identity, provenance,
time, and approval gates pass.

## Experience architecture

This is an evidence investigation. A sewer-overflow procedure, maintenance note, permit clause, and
sensor record disagree. The learner follows a passage through retrieval, extraction, identity
resolution, review, approval, and supersession without allowing format or similarity to decide truth.

## Signature mechanisms

The Document-to-Claim Promotion Rail assigns the next defensible state to five candidate items. The
Evidence-State Investigation classifies source documents, retrieved passages, extracted claims,
reviewed assertions, and governing claims.

## Work product

The Structured-Unstructured Evidence Plan records sources, access, retrieval, chunking, extraction,
identity, provenance, review, authority, effective time, conflict policy, retention, and promotion
tests.

## Evidence boundary

Embeddings, similarity, extraction confidence, and graph representation do not automatically create
approved enterprise knowledge.
