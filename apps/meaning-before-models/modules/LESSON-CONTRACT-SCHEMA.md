# Meaning Before Models Lesson Contract

Every planned module receives a `lesson-contract.yaml` before narrative, storyboard, visual, or
component production.

The contract must define:

- one learner question and one observable outcome;
- prerequisite knowledge, misconception, and transfer task;
- common, Practitioner, and Leader depth boundaries;
- one lesson archetype, opening situation, mental model, and signature mechanism;
- the explanatory jobs that visuals must perform, without selecting decorative assets;
- the cognitive jobs of assessments, without imposing a fixed quiz inventory;
- one useful work product and its contribution to the capstone;
- source and claim references, evidence limitations, prerequisites, and the next capability; and
- a visible approval state.

`draft_for_owner_review` means the lesson exists in the approved curriculum but does not authorize
storyboarding or implementation. `approved_for_design` permits a detailed module design brief and
storyboard. It does not approve compilation or release.
