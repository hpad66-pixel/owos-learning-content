# Module 11 Design Brief: References, Provenance, Authority, and Time

Status: approved for production under standing owner authorization

## Learning decision

The learner must decide which conflicting statement may support a bounded utility decision after
examining provenance, authority, observation time, record time, effective time, version, review,
supersession, and access.

## Experience architecture

This is an evidence-reconciliation room. Five credible claims arrive from telemetry, maintenance,
engineering, a retired procedure, and an extracted permit clause. Their record times and effective
times diverge. The learner builds evidence envelopes, places claims on dual timelines, and records a
governing statement without deleting the conflict.

## Signature mechanism

The Evidence Reconciliation Hearing presents five claims. The learner classifies how each was
produced, identifies its authority and effective interval, applies supersession policy, and chooses
the statement allowed for the decision.

## Work product

The Authority, Provenance, and Time Ledger records claim, source, production activity, responsible
agent, observation and record times, effective interval, version, authority scope, review state,
supersession, confidence, access boundary, and decision use.

## Evidence boundary

PROV-O supplies vocabulary for provenance. Authority and governing-use policy are organizational
decisions and are not assigned automatically by RDF, timestamps, confidence scores, or source
system names.
