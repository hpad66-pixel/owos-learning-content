# Module 06 Design Brief: Taxonomies, Vocabularies, and RDF Schema

Status: approved for production under standing owner authorization

## Learning decision

The learner must repair a utility classification without confusing a controlled vocabulary,
taxonomy, RDF Schema semantics, or SHACL validation.

## Experience architecture

This is a classification studio. Four departments place the word "asset" on different artifacts.
The learner sorts terms, classes, instances, and properties; walks a pump hierarchy; then repairs
domain, range, and subclass statements after seeing their inference consequences.

## Visual Arsenal selection

| Idea | Shape | Visual | Conclusion |

| Hierarchy inheritance | tree | Utility classification tree | Subclass membership carries upward |
| Domain and range consequences | cause map | Semantic consequence map | RDFS declarations can classify unexpected resources |

## Signature mechanism

The Hierarchy Consequence Laboratory asks the learner to accept, repair, or reject five schema
statements. Feedback shows the class-membership consequence of each choice.

## Work product

The Governed Vocabulary and Taxonomy Sheet records terms, definitions, hierarchy, properties,
semantic consequences, owner, release state, and a validation boundary.

## Evidence boundary

RDF Schema supplies vocabulary for classes, properties, subclass, subproperty, domain, and range.
Its domain and range declarations support inference. They are not equivalent to SHACL validation.
