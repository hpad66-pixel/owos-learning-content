# Module 09 Design Brief: Reasoning and Inference with OWL

Status: approved for production under standing owner authorization

## Learning decision

The learner must admit or reject an inferred utility statement after inspecting the asserted
evidence, declared axiom, proof path, profile boundary, and intended use.

## Experience architecture

This is a model courtroom. Assertions enter as evidence exhibits. An OWL axiom is read as the
declared rule. The reasoner presents a conclusion with a proof trace. The learner admits justified
entailments, rejects unsupported leaps, and distinguishes unknown from false.

## Signature mechanism

The OWL Model Court presents inverse, transitive, equivalent-class, class-membership, and
unsupported-leap cases. The learner admits, rejects, or holds each conclusion after opening its
assertions and axiom.

## Work product

The Inference Boundary Card records the assertions, axiom, entailed statement, proof requirement,
profile, limitations, allowed uses, prohibited uses, owner, and review route.

## Evidence boundary

OWL entailment follows declared formal semantics. It is not statistical prediction, language-model
generation, real-world verification, or permission to act.
