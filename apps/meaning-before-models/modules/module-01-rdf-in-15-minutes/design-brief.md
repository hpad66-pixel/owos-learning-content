## Learning job

The learner begins with a 2:00 a.m. pump alarm and must identify what the records still do not say.
The lesson converts an ordinary relationship into a subject, predicate, object statement, reverses
it to expose direction, and then joins three statements into an evidence path.

## Cognitive design

- Prior knowledge: learners already state ordinary relationships in speech.
- Misconception: more records automatically create machine understanding.
- Worked example: Pump P-104 serves Pressure Zone 3.
- Guided practice: build and reverse the pump triple.
- Retrieval: reveal subject, predicate, object, and triple flip cards.
- Relationship practice: trace the path from pump to service account.
- Transfer: create a wastewater or stormwater Relationship Card.
- Boundary: an explicit triple is a claim, not proof of truth or authority.
