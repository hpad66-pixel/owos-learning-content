# Module 13 Design Brief: Map Meaning to Data

Status: approved for production under standing owner authorization

## Learning decision

The learner must define, test, break, and repair a semantic mapping that connects an approved
concept to actual source fields without hiding identity, transformation, units, provenance, or
change ownership.

## Experience architecture

This is a semantic wiring workshop. Three systems expose a field named STATUS with three different
meanings. The learner separates the concepts, crosses a governed identity bridge, wires source
fields to graph terms, predicts the triples, breaks a mapping, and repairs the resulting answer.

## Signature mechanisms

The Source-to-Concept Wiring Bench maps status, identifiers, measurements, and relationships. The
Mapping Break and Repair Laboratory diagnoses renamed fields, unit drift, false identity matches,
and authority changes.

## Work product

The Semantic Mapping Specification records the competency question, source contract, identity rule,
target terms, transformation, expected triples, provenance, tests, owner, change trigger, and
failure behavior.

## Evidence boundary

A mapping can support query-time views or materialized RDF. Mapping is not synonymous with copying,
renaming columns, ordinary joining, or inferring facts.
