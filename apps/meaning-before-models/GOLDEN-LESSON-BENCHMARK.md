## What the benchmark governs

Every produced module must achieve an appropriate version of the following capabilities:

1. One explicit professional learning job.
2. Complete conversational teaching that works without video.
3. A consequential utility situation or question.
4. Explanatory visuals selected from the idea's natural shape.
5. One subject-specific signature mechanism.
6. Assessments selected by the thinking being taught.
7. Immediate explanatory feedback, retry, and debrief.
8. One reviewable professional artifact or a justified contribution to the accumulating fieldbook.
9. Water, wastewater, stormwater, or One Water transfer where relevant.
10. Controlled evidence, attribution, and authority boundaries.
11. Accessible structured source and deterministic compilation.
12. Rendered desktop, tablet, phone, keyboard, touch, reduced-motion, and read-without-video
    evidence, with human gates reported honestly.
13. A distinctiveness comparison against adjacent modules.

## What the benchmark does not govern

Future modules must not copy Module 05's:

- page arrangement;
- section count;
- visual count;
- decision-room archetype;
- color composition;
- Meaning Triage Desk;
- failure laboratory;
- assessment order;
- Zone 3 scenario; or
- Five-Layer Meaning Map interface.

Consistency means consistent quality and controls. It does not mean repeated lesson composition.

## Production test

For every future module, ask:

> Does this lesson achieve the benchmark's level of teaching clarity, usefulness, visual truth,
> accessibility, evidence control, and rendered reliability through an experience designed
> specifically for its own learning problem?

If the answer is no, the module is not ready for production review. If the answer is yes but its
composition substantially repeats an adjacent lesson without instructional justification, it is
also not ready.

## Remaining gates

Golden-benchmark approval does not approve:

- live replacement;
- release or deployment;
- graph publication;
- credentials;
- factual or practitioner review;
- novice comprehension review;
- human accessibility review; or
- operational authority.
