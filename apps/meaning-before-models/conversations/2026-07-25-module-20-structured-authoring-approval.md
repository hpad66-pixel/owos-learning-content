# Module 20 Structured Authoring Approval

Hardeep Anand approved the Module 20 Phase 0 design package and directed structured authoring and interaction prototyping.

The approval preserves:

- the fifteen-step sequence;
- wastewater, water, stormwater, and guided custom scenarios;
- the One Water Knowledge Spine Live Lab;
- the six Visual Arsenal systems;
- purposeful simulations and distributed assessments;
- the Portable One Water Knowledge Spine Use-Case Configuration;
- responsive desktop, iPad, and iPhone architecture;
- the Module 19 handoff;
- all evidence, security, authority, and non-production boundaries.

Hardeep explicitly directed that the module must not be published or marked final release approved during this phase.
