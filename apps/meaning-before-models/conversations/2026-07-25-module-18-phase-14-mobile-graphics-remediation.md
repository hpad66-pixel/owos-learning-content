# Module 18 Phase 14 Mobile Graphics Remediation

Hardeep identified during Phase 14 owner review that Module 18 graphics did not fit or teach
effectively in the Author Studio iPhone view. He directed a bounded remediation that preserves the
approved desktop experience, narrative, lesson contract, completion identifiers, assessments,
simulations, and evidence boundaries.

The remediation must:

- replace forced-width mobile graphics with complete fit-to-screen overviews;
- create mobile-specific vertical compositions for text-heavy graphics;
- provide an accessible full-screen detail viewer with zoom, pan, reset, close, focus return, and
  structured textual equivalents;
- update the permanent production contract and automated QA so containment alone cannot establish
  mobile readiness;
- retain individual desktop, iPad, iPhone, 200 percent, and 400 percent visual evidence;
- recompile and return the corrected working candidate to One Water OS Author Studio; and
- keep publication and final-release approval blocked.
