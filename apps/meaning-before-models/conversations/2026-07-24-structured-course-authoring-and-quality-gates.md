# Structured Course Authoring and Quality Gates

Date: 2026-07-24

Hardeep approved the recommendation to stop treating hand-authored HTML as the primary course
source and directed full implementation of a controlled module-by-module production system.

The approved direction includes:

- a structured Course Compiler source package;
- a module-level Author Studio;
- separate narrative, storyboard, visual, interaction, assessment, work-product, source, and QA
  artifacts;
- real visual assets rather than declared visual labels;
- storyboard, cognitive-quality, rendered-experience, and whole-course coherence gates;
- portability, analytics, localization, licensing, correction, and version controls; and
- conversion of RDF Module 01 as the first reference implementation.

HTML remains a delivery output. Existing published lessons remain unchanged until a compiled
replacement passes the new gates and receives release approval.
