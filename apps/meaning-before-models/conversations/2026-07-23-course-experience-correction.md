# Hardeep direction: course experience correction

Hardeep rejected course production that makes every course and lesson look the same. He specifically
rejected repeated archetypes, simulations, weak graphics, generic flip cards, fixed lesson
structures, and video-dependent instruction.

The required direction is:

- explain the material conversationally and in detail on the page;
- assume the written course may be the complete teaching experience;
- give each course its own identity;
- give each lesson graphics, simulations, questions, and work products appropriate to that lesson;
- fix Meaning Before Models so it can earn flagship status;
- correct the generation and quality system so the problem cannot recur; and
- apply the correction to Project Management, Data Before AI, Meaning Before Models, and future
  courses.
