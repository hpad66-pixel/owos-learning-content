# Fifteen-Minute RDF Course Direction

Date: 2026-07-22

Source: Hardeep Anand, typed directly in the Codex course-building conversation

Status: Preserved course direction. The course format and duration are approved direction. The detailed blueprint and lesson remain subject to review.

## Original direction

This will be like a 15-minute standalone course that we connect to the course on data, which we already have, and we'll all connect it later on. I think the concept here is about RDFs and breaking it down in the most simplistic manner, because meaning relationships kind of break it down to some simple thing that you start asking the user, the learner, by posing questions, so simplistically, in water, wastewater, and stormwater, one in every field.

Create this moment of "Wow, I never thought about it this way, the connectedness, the meanings, and so on," and then wrangle that complexity with the word data. We slowly are building the solutions using RDFs and why. What is RDF? Then you grow through that diagram that I'm pasting here all the way up to sparkle, then with examples, with animations on a computer or an iPhone or whatever iPad, to show exactly how that is done with knowledge graphs, semantics, references, inferences, etc.

All contained within the concept of this content as well as the diagram, the leaders have to go away with something that they should once and for all understand:

- the triple
- the triple RDF's differences
- real-life examples, at least as many as you can, so they finally get it into their head
