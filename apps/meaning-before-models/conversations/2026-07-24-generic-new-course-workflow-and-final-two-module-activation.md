# Generic new-course workflow and final-two-module activation

Date: 2026-07-24

Hardeep clarified that the activation prompt must remain completely generic. It must work for any
future topic and must not be tied to Meaning Before Models, Module 18, Module 19, or any predetermined
module count.

For a new topic, Hardeep expects to begin in the conversational Codex workspace by sparring,
supplying research, testing the point of view, and deciding what the learner should ultimately be
able to do. Research and course formulation precede syllabus and curriculum design. The curriculum
must then establish what comes first, second, third, and thereafter. The required number of modules
must emerge from the learning jobs and prerequisites.

Author Studio is a controlled module-review and editing surface after curriculum architecture and
lesson contracts exist. It is not the starting place for topic ideation, research, or module-count
selection.

Hardeep directed Codex to use this generic process for the next two Meaning Before Models modules.
The approved curriculum, nineteen lesson contracts, Module 05 capability benchmark, and existing
structured Modules 01 through 17 allow the run to begin at contract verification and module design
for Modules 18 and 19 without repeating completed research or curriculum approvals.
