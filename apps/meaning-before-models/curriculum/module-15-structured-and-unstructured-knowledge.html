02
Method comparison

Search, similarity, extraction, and graph links preserve different things

Keyword search finds exact terms. Vector similarity retrieves passages with related language. Extraction turns text into candidate entities, relationships, dates, and claims. Explicit graph assertions connect reviewed statements to identities and evidence.

These methods can work together. None of them makes a retrieved passage true or governing. A context engine should preserve the method, score, source locator, version, and access boundary.

Match each method to its primary job.

03
Document-to-Claim Promotion Rail

A passage earns controls one gate at a time

The promotion rail begins with a source document and a located passage. Extraction creates a candidate claim. Identity resolution connects named things to governed resources. Review checks meaning and evidence. Approval assigns a scoped use and effective interval.

Move each item only to the next defensible state. If source, identity, or authority is missing, hold it rather than manufacturing certainty.

04
Evidence-state investigation

Representation and evidence state are separate questions

The same sentence can exist as text, an embedding vector, an extracted candidate, an RDF statement, or an approved claim. Representation describes how software stores or accesses it. Evidence state describes what review and authority support its use.

Classify each artifact by evidence state. A graph triple can still be unreviewed. A document clause can be governing when the correct version, scope, and authority are established.

05
Conflict network

Approval does not require deleting disagreement

Suppose the procedure says start Pump B, the maintenance record says Pump B is unavailable, and the permit requires notification. A reviewed decision can hold the start instruction for this incident while keeping all three claims and their provenance.

Record the conflict, chosen governing use, missing evidence, reviewer, effective interval, and supersession. That history supports audit and future correction.

    Questions people actually ask

    Is unstructured content the same as dark data?

    No. Unstructured describes form. Dark data is a business label for data that is poorly known or used. Do not assume a universal percentage.

    Does an embedding contain the meaning of a document?

    It encodes patterns useful for similarity retrieval. It does not preserve every statement, rule, source boundary, or authority decision.

    When may an extracted sentence enter the graph?

    It can enter as a candidate claim with source provenance. Governing use requires the review and authority policy for that claim.

    Can artificial intelligence approve its own extraction?

    It can assist checks, but approval authority must be assigned by policy and risk.

    What happens when the source document changes?

    Re-index or re-extract under version control, retain the prior evidence interval, run regression tests, and notify affected consumers.

    What should happen to low-confidence candidates?

    Hold, route, or exclude them according to policy. Do not silently promote them.

    Evidence boundary

    Retrieval and extraction methods provide candidate evidence. Approval, authority, effective time, and governing use are organizational controls and are not conferred by similarity scores or RDF representation.

    Module Glossary

    unstructured content

    Content whose main meaning is carried in prose images audio or other media rather than fixed fields.

    embedding

    A numeric representation used to compare similarity for retrieval.

    retrieval

    Selecting source items or passages relevant to a request.

    extraction

    Turning content into candidate entities relationships values or claims.

    candidate claim

    A machine- or human-proposed statement that has not completed required review and approval.

    evidence promotion

    A controlled change from one evidence state to another after defined gates pass.
