# Module Design Brief: Structured and Unstructured Knowledge

## Identity

| Field | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Course and module ID | `owos-course-semantic-data-ai-001`, `mbm001:15` |
| Archetype | Evidence investigation |
| Signature mechanism | Promote one passage through five evidence states |
| Work product | Structured-Unstructured Evidence Plan |
| Evidence boundary | Fictional Lift Station 7 conflict; review authority is utility-specific |

## Learning job

Learners investigate a conflict among an asset row, approved procedure, and maintenance note. They
must preserve disagreement, separate retrieval from authority, promote evidence gate by gate, and
design a traceable integration plan.

## Concept-to-experience plan

| Idea | Visual | Action | Result | Quiz type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Form is not truth | `editorial-illustration` | Judge the case | Conflict remains visible | `evidence-judgment` |
| Retrieval is not approval | `provenance-network` | Promote five states in order | Evidence chain accumulates | `evidence-promotion-sequence` |
| Representation has a job | `comparison-table` | Classify a maintenance note | Candidate remains separate from fact | `evidence-state-classification` |
| Traceable reuse | Artifact preview | Specify source, locator, reviewer, time | Reviewable plan | `provenance-trace` |

## Module design fingerprint

- Narrative: case file, evidence rail, representation board, investigation closeout.
- Purposeful interactions: five-gate promotion and maintenance-note evidence classifier.
- Visuals: `editorial-illustration`, `provenance-network`, `comparison-table`.
- Assessment sequence: `evidence-judgment`, `evidence-promotion-sequence`,
  `evidence-state-classification`, `provenance-trace`.
- Opening uses conflicting artifacts, unlike Module 14's incident clock and Module 16's two-shift
  ambiguity.
- Mobile stacks the paper evidence and promotion rail; reduced motion uses selected states.

## Instructor explanation plan

The instructor first prevents voting by format, then narrates what each evidence gate adds. The
representation table is read by job, retained evidence, and unsafe shortcut. The closeout debrief
keeps uncertainty visible.

## Written-first review

The lesson defines structured data, unstructured content, claim, provenance, embedding, retrieval,
extraction, and entity in context. It explains why rows, documents, retrieved passages, extracted
candidates, and reviewed graph statements have different evidence status.

## Visual pacing review

The case scene breaks the opening explanation. The promotion rail and live gate mechanism alternate
with short teaching. The comparison table precedes the classifier. No video carries required content.

## Explanatory graphic plan

The case-file illustration exposes authority and time. The provenance network shows accumulated
evidence. The comparison table separates source document, search index, candidate, and reviewed graph
statement.

## Learner FAQ plan

Six case-room questions cover dark data, embeddings, sentence extraction, AI approval, document
change, and low-confidence candidates.

## Recording script

Optional only. The written lesson is the complete instruction.

## Diversity check

Adjacent Module 14 optimizes access constraints. Module 15 investigates evidence status through a
five-state rail, paper case file, and classifier. Module 16 switches to packet assembly and failure
controls. Interaction signatures and quiz sequences are unique.
