## Learning job

The lesson opens on a two-second main-break response target. Learners must reject one universal
access pattern, stress latency, freshness, outage, and source type, then record authority, ownership,
and failure behavior. The work product is an Access-Pattern Decision Record.

## Module design fingerprint

- Lesson archetype: constraint stress test.
- Signature mechanism: change access constraints and defend a pattern.
- Narrative architecture: incident clock, decision surface, failure rehearsal, decision record.
- Purposeful interactions: weighted access-pattern stress test and stale-copy control diagnosis.
- Visuals: `editorial-illustration`, `utility-estate-map`, `failure-propagation-chain`.
- Assessment sequence: `scenario-choice`, `constraint-stress-test`, `control-diagnosis`,
  `architecture-defense`.
- Work product: source-by-source Access-Pattern Decision Record.
- Distinctive pacing: narrow incident scene, wide stress board, narrow failure rehearsal, artifact.
- Graph and Community: accessible side drawers with focus return through the shared fieldbook runtime.
- Mobile: decision boards stack; reduced motion preserves directly selectable states.

## Written-first review

The page teaches every term before the simulations, includes a worked P-104 relationship, and remains
complete without video. It explicitly corrects the belief that virtualization means no integration
and that moving the least data is an absolute rule.

## Visual pacing review

No more than two prose blocks occur without a decision, visual, simulation, debrief, or artifact.
Dark output surfaces use explicit light text. Mobile layouts become one column.

## Explanatory graphic plan

The incident illustration teaches competing clocks. The utility estate map teaches source-specific
routes. The failure-propagation chain teaches why hidden staleness becomes operational risk. Every
visual has a reading guide and learner conclusion.

## Learner FAQ plan

Six questions address virtualization work, cache safety, indexes versus authority, materialization
criteria, mixed-pattern architectures, and the meaning of moving the least data.

## Recording script

No recording is required for course completion. The existing optional script is not used as
conformance evidence.

## Diversity check

Module 13 is a mapping workshop. Module 14 changes to an incident-clock constraint stress test. Its
opening, three visual sequence, two interaction mechanisms, quiz sequence, and decision-record
format differ from adjacent Modules 13 and 15.
