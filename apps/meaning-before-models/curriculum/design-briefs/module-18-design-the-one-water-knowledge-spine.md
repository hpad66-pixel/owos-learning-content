# Module Design Brief: Design the One Water Knowledge Spine

## Identity

| Field | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Course and module ID | `owos-course-semantic-data-ai-001`, `mbm001:18` |
| Archetype | Capstone design review |
| Signature mechanism | Build, score, and defend a ninety-day pilot |
| Work product | Ninety-Day One Water Pilot Canvas |
| Evidence boundary | Instructional P-104 pilot; no deployment authority |

## Learning job

Learners reject an unbounded enterprise-graph proposal, assemble a minimum viable Knowledge Spine,
score the pilot before a review panel, and produce a fourteen-decision ninety-day canvas.

## Concept-to-experience plan

| Idea | Visual | Action | Result | Quiz type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scale is not value | `editorial-illustration` | Challenge scope | Missing governance becomes visible | `scope-challenge` |
| Spine is a layered capability | `layered-stack` | Assemble required capabilities | Bounded vertical slice appears | `capability-portfolio-selection` |
| Outcomes need owners | `raci-grid` | Score five dimensions | Weak governance or safety blocks | `weighted-pilot-review` |
| Pilot must be defensible | Artifact preview | Complete fourteen decisions | Reviewable ninety-day plan | `capstone-defense` |

## Module design fingerprint

- Narrative: executive rejection, architecture table, review panel, ninety-day defense.
- Purposeful interactions: capability portfolio selection and five-dimension weighted review.
- Visuals: `editorial-illustration`, `layered-stack`, `raci-grid`.
- Assessment sequence: `scope-challenge`, `capability-portfolio-selection`,
  `weighted-pilot-review`, `capstone-defense`.
- Work product: large capstone canvas, not a repeated short form.
- Mobile stacks the proposal, layers, panel, and measure board; reduced motion preserves all states.

## Instructor explanation plan

The instructor first reveals blank governance fields in the “all data” proposal. The cutaway is read
from source to consumption. The review panel explicitly blocks low governance or safety scores. The
canvas debrief requires measures, boundaries, non-goals, and stop criteria.

## Written-first review

The capstone defines Knowledge Spine, ontology, pilot, competency question, identifiers, mappings,
shapes, and provenance through the P-104 exposure question. A written ninety-day sequence remains
usable without media.

## Visual pacing review

The executive scene, layered cutaway, portfolio assembler, measure board, review panel, and canvas
break every dense segment. Dark verdict surfaces use light text.

## Explanatory graphic plan

The proposal scene teaches why scale can be empty. The layered stack teaches architecture. The RACI
measure board ties operational, semantic, service, and safety results to accountable owners.

## Learner FAQ plan

Seven sponsor questions cover platform timing, ontology size, first use case, lakehouse coexistence,
ownership, demo avoidance, and stop conditions.

## Recording script

Optional only. The written capstone contains the complete learning and review process.

## Diversity check

Module 18 intentionally changes rhythm from laboratory to executive review. It has Roman-numbered
stages, a layered cutaway, portfolio assembler, weighted panel, and fourteen-field capstone. Its quiz
and interaction signatures are unique.
