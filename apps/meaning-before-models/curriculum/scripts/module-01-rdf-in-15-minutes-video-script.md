# Module 01 Recording Script: RDF in 15 Minutes

Status: production candidate

## Scene 1
### Spoken words
A pump alarm appears at 2:00 a.m. The records name a pump, a pressure zone, a work order, and customers, but no record states the complete path.

Before we reach for a technical term, make the decision. Which statement gives a machine one reusable piece of meaning?

[Show the opening decision and pause for the learner.]

## Scene 2
### Spoken words
Here is the mental model. Resource Description Framework starts with one directed, named relationship: subject, predicate, object.

We will move through four steps: Name the subject, Choose the relationship, Name the object, Connect the next triple.

### Visual direction
[Reveal the four selected graphics one at a time. State where to look first and what each proves.]

## Scene 3
### Spoken words

answer is wrong, use the explanation and retry.
