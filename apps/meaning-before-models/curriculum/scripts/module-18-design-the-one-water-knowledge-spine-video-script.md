# Module 18 Recording Script: Design the One Water Knowledge Spine

Status: production candidate

## Scene 1
### Spoken words
A team proposes an enterprise knowledge graph before naming one operational question, one decision owner, or one measurable outcome.

Before we reach for a technical term, make the decision. What should the pilot define first?

### Visual direction
[Show the opening decision and pause for the learner.]

## Scene 2
### Spoken words
Here is the mental model. A useful pilot connects graph fit, identity, relationships, evidence, mappings, validation, context, access, agent controls, ownership, and measures around one decision.

We will move through four steps: Bound the question, Run the Graph Fit Test, Design controls and evidence, Plan ninety-day measures and stop conditions.

### Visual direction
[Reveal the four selected graphics one at a time. State where to look first and what each proves.]

## Scene 3
### Spoken words
Now operate the mechanism. Select each step, then match the terms to the jobs they perform. If an
answer is wrong, use the explanation and retry.

### Visual direction
[Demonstrate the mechanism laboratory and one matching pair.]

## Scene 4
### Spoken words
Build the One Water Knowledge Spine Pilot Canvas. Be specific enough that another utility team can review the sources,
relationships, controls, owners, and human authority boundary.

### Visual direction
[Show the artifact builder, saved preview, and applied criteria.]

## Scene 5
### Spoken words
The takeaway is simple. A useful pilot connects graph fit, identity, relationships, evidence, mappings, validation, context, access, agent controls, ownership, and measures around one decision. The technology can support the work. A named human remains
accountable for the utility decision.

### Visual direction
[Open the lesson Graph, then point to the next module.]
