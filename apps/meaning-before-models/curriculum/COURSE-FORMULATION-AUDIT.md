## What should be retained

1. The title and central argument: enterprise data does not acquire shared meaning automatically.
2. The learning progression from one relationship to a governed semantic and agentic application.
3. The Pump P-104 continuity case, with wastewater and stormwater transfer cases.
4. The distinction among assertion, inference, validation, provenance, approval, and generated text.
5. The explicit comparison of tables and Business Intelligence, Retrieval-Augmented Generation,
   graph retrieval, context assembly, and agent action.
6. The bounded-pilot capstone rather than an enterprise-graph transformation promise.
7. The standards-first evidence boundary and the warnings against categorical technology claims.

## What needs revision before lesson production

### 1. The primary learner is not singular enough

The current syllabus names nontechnical staff, operators, engineers, managers, executives, board
members, data practitioners, Geographic Information System practitioners, operational technology,
information technology, cybersecurity, analytics, and artificial-intelligence practitioners.
Serving all of them as equal primary learners makes depth and pacing unstable.

### 2. The graduation capability needs one plain statement

The current fourteen program outcomes are useful, but they do not yet reduce to one observable
graduate performance that controls every lesson.

### 3. Module 01 overlaps later mastery lessons

The fifteen-minute primer previews triples, graph growth, the World Wide Web Consortium stack,
querying, inference, and validation. Its job must remain orientation and recognition. Modules 02,
04, 08, 09, and 10 must carry the actual mastery so the course does not teach the same explanation
twice.

### 4. Foundation, Practitioner, and Leader depth is not yet allocated

The views are named, but the syllabus does not consistently identify what belongs on the common
route, what is optional implementation depth, and what changes only for a leadership decision.

### 5. The work-product progression may be too large

Eighteen artifacts can become eighteen forms. The progression should be reorganized into a smaller
number of accumulating sections of one Utility Knowledge Spine Fieldbook, with lesson-specific
artifacts only when the professional job genuinely differs.

### 6. Several load-bearing claims still need review

Ontology as a universal requirement for agentic artificial intelligence, enterprise adoption,
determinism comparisons, grounding claims, and product-specific architecture claims remain limited,
proposed, or owner positions. These boundaries must be assigned to specific lessons before the
lesson contracts are approved.

### 7. Approval records conflict

The approval register records the eighteen-module blueprint as approved, while parts of the syllabus
still describe it as a draft. The revalidation must produce one superseding blueprint status.

## Recommended course formulation

### Primary learner

An intelligent utility professional or leader who uses data and must make or govern decisions, but
does not begin with semantic-technology or programming experience.

### Secondary learner

Data, Geographic Information System, information-technology, operational-technology, analytics,
governance, and artificial-intelligence practitioners who need optional implementation depth and a
shared vocabulary with the primary learner.

### Graduation capability

Given one bounded utility question, the learner can identify the important entities and
relationships, distinguish asserted from inferred or validated statements, trace an answer to
evidence, choose an appropriate semantic access pattern, and define the context and controls needed
before an application or agent may use or act on the result.

### What the learner is not required to do

- Become an ontology engineer
- Memorize Resource Description Framework serializations
- Write advanced SPARQL Protocol and RDF Query Language queries
- Configure a production graph database
- Treat every enterprise-data problem as a graph problem
- Permit an artificial-intelligence agent to act autonomously

### Mastery evidence

The learner produces and defends one bounded One Water Knowledge Spine Pilot Canvas supported by a
smaller accumulating fieldbook of relationships, identities, meaning, query, validation,
provenance, mapping, context, and action controls.
