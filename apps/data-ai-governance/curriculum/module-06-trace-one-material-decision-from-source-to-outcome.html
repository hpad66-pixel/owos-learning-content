Trace one material decision from source to outcome

A trusted answer is only the middle of the story. Follow it backward through every source and transformation, then forward through approval, action, outcome, correction, and learning. Preserve enough proof that another qualified person can reconstruct what happened without guessing.

Riverbend, reconstruct the monthly operating report

A filed monthly operating report includes a high wet-weather flow, a bypass note, laboratory results, and a compliance explanation. The final portable document format file looks complete. Riverbend must still trace it to supervisory control and data acquisition (SCADA) readings, laboratory records, calculations, spreadsheet adjustments, reconciliation, sign-off, the resulting operating action, and the later correction. A missing workbook formula version could change the reported total and the response.

The trace rule

For every material result, preserve four connected views: what it came from, what changed it, who or what acted, and what happened next. A diagram without run evidence is incomplete. A signed report without source and transformation evidence is also incomplete.

A trace is a chain of evidence. You should be able to point from the final answer back to its source and forward to the action and outcome.

Record identities, versions, inputs, outputs, activities, agents, times, code or configuration, approvals, use limits, exceptions, and retention at each material handoff.

Choose trace depth by consequence. Require complete and tested evidence for regulatory, safety, service, capital, customer-rights, and high-impact model decisions.

1 / 3

Trace backward from the answer

Data lineage shows the route from sources through transformations to a result. Provenance explains the production history of each important record. The World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) PROV family gives a useful pattern: entities are generated or used by activities, and activities are associated with agents. Riverbend can apply that pattern without buying a particular product.

Begin with the filed report and move backward. Identify its exact version and filing receipt. Find the approved report package, workbook, extraction run, laboratory batch, historian interval, instrument, sample, asset, and location. At each step ask which record is authoritative for the specific fact. An authoritative source is approved for a particular fact and time, not automatically for every field.

2 / 3

Trace forward to action, outcome, and correction

A complete chain explains what the answer caused. Link the report or model result to the authorized decision, the action taken, the expected outcome, the observed outcome, any issue, the correction, and what changed afterward. Otherwise Riverbend may know how a number was calculated but not whether it led to safer or more reliable service.

A correction must not erase history. Preserve the previous version, the reason, approval, effective time, affected decisions and consumers, notices, and verification. If the report total changes, Riverbend should be able to identify which regulatory record, dashboard, vendor model, management briefing, and operating decision consumed the earlier value.

3 / 3

Govern every handoff, including spreadsheets

A handoff is where meaning and proof often fall away. Every material handoff should carry the record identity and version, meaning and units, quality state, approved purpose, use limits, source and transformation history, responsible role, receipt or acceptance, exceptions, and challenge evidence.

Spreadsheets, email attachments, pasted values, phone calls, handwritten logs, and vendor portals are part of the real chain. Include them in lineage or place them in a controlled exception process. A spreadsheet can be governed when its owner, purpose, inputs, outputs, formula and macro versions, access, change review, reconciliation, release, retention, and successor are controlled. Calling it temporary does not reduce the consequence of a wrong value.

Chapter 06 evidence gate

  • The trace begins with one exact material result, version, claim, scope, time, purpose, consequence, and authority.
  • Backward lineage reaches identified authoritative sources through every material automated and manual transformation.
  • Provenance records inputs, outputs, activity or run, actor or service, time, and code or configuration version.
  • Forward lineage connects approval, action, outcome, issue, correction, affected consumers, and learning.
  • Every material handoff preserves identity, meaning, quality state, purpose, limits, provenance, receipt, exceptions, and challenge.
  • Broken links have consequence, containment, owner, repair, due date, validation, and residual-risk authority.
  • A qualified alternate can reproduce the result and explain the decision boundary using retained, protected evidence.

Leader's call

Ask for one complete trace of a filed report, safety decision, capital recommendation, customer-impacting action, or model result. A successful enterprise catalog demo does not substitute for a qualified alternate reconstructing a real decision and its outcome.
