# Course Experience Brief: Data Before AI

Status: Chapter 09 benchmark preserved, unfinished chapters require distinct production plans

## Course identity

| Decision | Course-specific answer |
| --- | --- |
| Central argument | A utility cannot govern artificial intelligence without governing the data, decisions, authority, evidence, and accountability that feed it. |
| Learner transformation | Move from seeing governance as policy documentation to operating it around material utility decisions. |
| Conversational teaching voice | A governance lead working beside operators, engineers, technology teams, data stewards, legal reviewers, suppliers, and executives. |
| Utility world | Riverbend and other utility decisions spanning supervisory control, geographic information, customer, work, laboratory, supplier, document, and analytical records. |
| Visual language | Evidence desk, record journey, lineage room, applicability gate, responsibility forum, control boundary, quality profile, and assurance board. |
| Signature learning mechanisms | Applicability determination, estate mapping, identity resolution, decision trace, authority simulation, lineage reconstruction, quality-by-use testing, resilience challenge, privacy-rights review, retention decision, supplier boundary, operational-technology control, artificial-intelligence evidence gate, assessment scoring, roadmap funding, and ninety-day launch. |
| Professional artifact family | A governed utility data and artificial-intelligence operating pack. |
| Prohibited motifs | Cloning Chapter 09, repeating artifact-builder pages, using static policy cards where a decision or evidence path should be operated, and publishing destination shells as finished lessons. |

## Written-first teaching contract

- Explain governance in ordinary utility language before introducing frameworks.
- Work every domain through a material decision and its evidence.
- Distinguish policy, operating control, record, proof, and assurance.
- Let learners inspect and change the evidence path, not merely read about it.
- Keep the Foundation, Practitioner, and Leader views conversational and complete without recordings.

## Production decision

Chapter 09 remains the capability benchmark, not the layout. Chapters 10 to 24 require individual
archetypes and signature mechanisms before they are built. Destination shells are not lessons and
cannot satisfy course completeness. The released chapters must also pass the new whole-course
distinctiveness audit before final release.
