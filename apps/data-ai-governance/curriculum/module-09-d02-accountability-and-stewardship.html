The utility situation

Everyone contributed. Nobody owned the joined truth.

Riverbend must submit its lead-service-line inventory. The geographic information system, customer system, inspections, contractor files, and regulatory reporting database all contain part of the answer.

The deadline is Friday

Engineering owns the map. Customer service owns the account record. A contractor completed field inspections. Information technology operates the integration. Regulatory affairs must sign the submission. Each team can explain its own file, but no named person can approve the joined data product, resolve conflicts, or accept the risk of an unknown classification.

The problem is not cooperation. The problem is the absence of accountable authority across the handoffs.

Section 1 | D02.1 and D02.2

Name the authority, not merely the person.

A name in a catalog is useful only when the role has a defined purpose, authority, time, competence, measures, and escalation route.

Accountable ownerApproves purpose, use, risk acceptance, service level, and material exceptions.
Operational stewardMaintains definitions, rules, metadata, issue resolution, and consumer communication.
Technical custodianOperates platforms, access, backup, integration, and technical controls.
Data consumerUses the product only for approved decisions and reports defects or misuse.
Independent assuranceTests design and operation without becoming responsible for management's control.

Section 2 | D02.3

Build a forum that makes decisions.

A governance forum is not a standing meeting with slides. It is a controlled route for decisions that cross operations, engineering, security, privacy, legal, records, procurement, analytics, and suppliers.

Issue arrivesNamed decision, consequence, deadline, and evidence owner
TriageAuthority, materiality, affected rights, systems, and obligations
AdviceRequired operational, technical, legal, security, and records views
DecisionNamed decision owner, rationale, limits, dissent, and residual risk
Follow-throughAction, due date, evidence, communication, retest, and closure

Foundation view

Ask one question: who can say yes, no, not yet, or escalate for this data product?

Practitioner view

Make authority executable through role profiles, thresholds, decision logs, issue workflows, and evidence that survives staff changes.

Leader view

Fund stewardship as operating capacity. A title without time, competence, measures, and escalation authority is not a control.

Section 3 | D02.4 and D02.5

Separate conflicting duties and keep suppliers accountable.

The same person or supplier should not quietly create, approve, release, change, and assure a consequential data product. Efficient handoffs still require independent challenge where the risk demands it.
