## Course-level review

- Chapters 10 through 15 use six different archetypes, visual sequences, quiz types, and artifact formats.
- No adjacent retrofit lesson shares the same opening, dominant visual, interaction signature, or quiz type.
- Shared drawers, navigation, responsive behavior, evidence controls, and completion semantics are intentional.
- Chapters 16 through 24 remain release blockers until their destination shells are replaced and the complete course passes distinctiveness.
