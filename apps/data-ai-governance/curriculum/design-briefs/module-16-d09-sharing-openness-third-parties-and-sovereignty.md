## Learning job

The learner must stop an underspecified smart-meter transfer, preserve the proposed benefit, and write a sharing record that follows the data from purpose through exit.

## Instructor explanation plan

| Component | Instructor job | Debrief |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sharing boundary map | Explain that classification changes with purpose, identity, detail, recipient, place, and time | Category is never permanent |
| Supplier gate board | Walk left to right from authority to exit | A later pass cannot erase an earlier stop |
| Priority check | Reinforce purpose before technical convenience | Accuracy of the sequence matters |

## Visual pacing review

- Negotiation board and worked example break the opening teaching.
- The two visual mechanisms are separated by supplier, sovereignty, and exit explanation.
- A five-part contract strip gives a compact review before assessment.
- Mobile presentation stacks the negotiation and matrix; reduced motion preserves all states.

## Learner FAQ plan

Six questions cover open data, de-identification, supplier ownership language, sovereignty, exit testing, and product-training reuse. Each answer states a practical utility boundary.
