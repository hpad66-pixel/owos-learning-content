# Module Design Brief: Chapter 03

## Identity

Course `owos-master-data-governance-001`; learning object `dga001:03`; **Standards, law, and the applicability record**. Controlled anchors are Version 2.3 Chapters 3 and 7. The course does not provide legal advice.

## Learning job

The learner determines whether cited authorities can support Riverbend's cloud leak-detection pilot and builds a controlled applicability record.

| Idea | Shape | Visual | Action | Result | Quiz |

## Module design fingerprint

Jurisdiction review desk. Signature mechanism is the applicability gate before scoring. Existing authority stack, standards table, classification, legal evidence check, interactive gate, change timeline, register builder, and tracker remain. Work product is the authority and applicability register.

## Instructor explanation plan

The learner is shown how force, purpose, incorporation, scope, effective date, and reviewer differ. Feedback requires the narrowest supportable claim.

## Written-first review

More than 1,200 words and the cloud-pilot worked example stand without video. “Review needed” is taught as a valid controlled outcome.

## Visual pacing review

Authority stack, swimlane, applicability gate, timeline, builder, and tracker break prose. Dark surfaces use light text.

## Explanatory graphic plan

The `layered-stack` separates authority types. The `applicability-gate` prevents scoring before facts and authority are verified. Mobile preserves the ordered gates.

## Learner FAQ plan

Five questions address standards versus law, course limits, edition control, uncertainty, and review cadence.

## Recording script

Not required.
