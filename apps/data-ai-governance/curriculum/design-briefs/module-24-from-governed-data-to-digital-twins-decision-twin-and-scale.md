## Learning job

The learner distinguishes capability phases, translates public patterns into internal proof, operates a continuous-assurance scale cycle, and defends a bounded Decision Twin scale brief.

## Concept-to-experience plan

| Teaching idea | Visual | Learner action | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Readiness dimensions stay separate | `radar-chart` | Inspect the weakest evidence | `evidence-supported-scale-defense` |
| Scale is a repeated assurance cycle | `cycle-loop` | Operate the next-use loop | Applied brief defense |

## Module design fingerprint

- Signature mechanism: defend the path from governed data to Decision Twin.
- Opening: a successful bounded pilot triggers an unsupported enterprise claim.
- Narrative: hearing, phase decision, public-case translation, readiness review, scale cycle, panel defense.
- Purposeful interactions: capability-phase evidence hearing and continuous-assurance scale cycle.
- Visual sequence: `phase-gate`, `before-after-slider`, `radar-chart`, `cycle-loop`.
- Quiz sequence: `capability-phase-classification`, `public-claim-to-internal-proof`, `evidence-supported-scale-defense`, final defense.
- Work-product mode: scale and Decision Twin brief.
- FAQ: six scale-panel questions.

## Instructor explanation plan

The hearing explanation increases evidence with the claim. The claim funnel separates transferable questions from local proof. The readiness explanation prohibits averaging critical dimensions. The scale-cycle explanation connects reuse with local verification, outcome observation, assurance, and stop rules.

## Visual pacing review

The panel scenario, worked eight-station decision, four distinct graphics, three assessments, role views, and final brief prevent prose clustering. Mobile stacks the hearing and funnel. Reduced motion retains direct state changes and all labels.

## Learner FAQ plan

Questions address dashboards versus twins, Decision Twin meaning, whether the highest phase is necessary, public cases, scale stop conditions, and Knowledge Graph reuse.

## Recording script

Not planned. The written panel hearing is complete without media.
