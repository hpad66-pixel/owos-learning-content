# Module Design Brief: Chapter 07

## Identity

Course `owos-master-data-governance-001`; learning object `dga001:07`; **Roles, stewardship, forums, and the operating rhythm**. Controlled anchors are Version 2.3 role and Figure 8 sections.

## Learning job

The learner routes a cross-domain exception, separates incompatible duties, and builds a governance operating model that survives staff changes.

## Module design fingerprint

Role-based case conference. Signature mechanism is routing an overdue exception through written thresholds. Existing handoff, responsibility table, sorter, decision route, forum builder, rhythm, role pack, and tracker remain. Work product is the governance operating model.

## Instructor explanation plan

The learner is taught to classify actions by authority, not title, and to identify creation, approval, release, risk, and assurance conflicts.

## Written-first review

More than 1,100 words and the billing-meter exception worked example remain without media.

## Visual pacing review

Swimlane, grid, route simulation, process rhythm, builders, and evidence gate break prose. Dark surfaces use light text.

## Explanatory graphic plan

The `swimlane` shows changing responsibility. The `raci-grid` separates accountable authority from support. Mobile converts both to ordered role cards.

## Learner FAQ plan

Five questions cover combined roles, forum authority, operational stewardship, cadence, and supplier accountability.

## Recording script

Not required.

## Diversity check

This role conference differs from Chapter 06's evidence replay and Chapter 08's investment room. Its scenario decision and operating-model artifact are unique.
