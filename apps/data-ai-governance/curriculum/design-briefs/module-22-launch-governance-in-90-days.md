# OWOS Module Design Brief

## Identity

| Field | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Course and module ID | `owos-master-data-governance-001`, `dga001:22` |
| Working title | Do not launch a framework. Launch a working cycle. |
| Archetype | Ninety-day launch simulation |
| Controlled sources | Method v2.3 Chapter 11; syllabus Chapter 22 |
| Evidence boundary | No appointment, labor, spending, or program authority |

## Learning job

The learner mobilizes a bounded cross-functional team around three to five material decisions, operates a weekly evidence ritual, and produces a ready-to-run 90-day launch plan.

## Concept-to-experience plan

| Teaching idea | Visual | Learner action | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thirteen weeks create an operating sequence | `timeline-roadmap` | Rehearse each week | `launch-sequencing` |
| Different decisions reuse governance at different thresholds | `heat-grid` | Compare five decision cards | `scope-creep-defense` |
| Governance recurs through evidence | `cycle-loop` | Operate the weekly ritual | `executive-launch-decision` |
| Leaders need decisions, not vanity metrics | `gauge-dial` | Read critical status separately | Applied launch defense |

## Module design fingerprint

- Signature mechanism: staff and sequence the first governance operating cycle.
- Opening: sponsor requests an enterprise inventory before selecting decisions.
- Narrative: mobilize, rehearse, trace, operate weekly, decide and fund.
- Purposeful interactions: thirteen-week launch rehearsal and weekly evidence ritual.
- Visual sequence: `timeline-roadmap`, `heat-grid`, `cycle-loop`, `gauge-dial`.
- Quiz sequence: `launch-sequencing`, `scope-creep-defense`, `executive-launch-decision`, final defense.
- Work-product mode: 90-day launch plan.
- FAQ: six mobilization questions.

## Instructor explanation plan

The ribbon explanation connects each week to an evidence record and next decision. The portfolio explanation preserves use-specific consequences. The ritual explanation distinguishes meeting decisions from work between meetings. The dashboard explanation blocks critical-condition averaging.

## Visual pacing review

The opening, worked setup, ribbon, portfolio, ritual, dashboard, distributed assessments, role views, and plan form alternate reading and action. Mobile uses horizontal labeled strips or stacked cards. Reduced motion preserves all content and live state.

## Explanatory graphic plan

| Graphic | Learner conclusion | Accessible treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thirteen-week ribbon | Fast work alternates scope, evidence, controls, and decisions | Buttons and live week detail |
| Portfolio heat grid | One threshold does not fit every decision | Semantic cards |
| Weekly cycle | Exceptions reach authority and retest | Keyboard-operable loop |
| Leader dashboard | Critical red conditions remain separate | Text labels and values |

## Learner FAQ plan

Questions address decision count, leadership roles, protected time, policy-first launches, day-90 deliverables, and avoiding an IT-only program.

## Recording script

Not planned. The complete instruction and simulation are on page.

## Diversity check

Chapter 21 is an investment room. Chapter 22 uses a calendar simulation, portfolio heat grid, ritual loop, and staffing plan. Chapter 23 changes to a product studio. Quiz and interaction signatures do not repeat adjacent lessons.
