# Module Design Brief: Chapter 00

## Identity

Course `owos-master-data-governance-001`; learning object `dga001:00`; working title **How to use the Master Class**. Learners are utility staff, practitioners, and leaders. Controlled source is *Data Ready Before AI Ready*, Version 2.3. The lesson teaches course use and evidence boundaries, not certification, legal advice, or assurance.

## Learning job

The learner chooses one material decision, distinguishes six claim levels, and establishes an evidence-safe Launch Pack. Completion evidence is the decision statement, source baseline, role map, claims register, workspace rule, and artifact review.

## Module design fingerprint

Orientation workshop; signature mechanism is choosing the decision that carries the course. Narrative moves from boundary to decision to workspace. The existing roadmap, gate process, claim classification, decision recommender, role tree, method, builder, and tracker remain distinct. Work product is the Launch Pack index. Graph and Community stay on the page through focus-return drawers. Header includes Graph, Community, and Start. The bottom `#owos-course-community` anchor precedes navigation. Motion is instructional only; reduced motion preserves all labels. Mobile changes the roadmap and fields to one or two columns. Existing local persistence and completion events remain.

## Instructor explanation plan

The roadmap is taught left to right. The four gates are explained as different products with different authority and evidence. The claim check explains retry reasoning. The Launch Pack is introduced as a governed utility workspace, not browser storage.

## Written-first review

Approximately 1,200 visible teaching words, a Riverbend wet-weather worked example, explicit claim boundaries, and complete written explanations remain without video.

## Visual pacing review

Roadmap, comparison table, phase-gate process, decision recommender, role tree, work-product fields, and tracker break the prose. Dark surfaces use light text. No editorial illustration is required for this orientation lesson.

## Explanatory graphic plan

The `timeline-roadmap` proves the capstone is assembled across the course. The `phase-gate` proves method publication, pilot, application, and independent review are separate decisions. Both include reading guides, conclusions, responsive behavior, and text equivalents.

## Learner FAQ plan

Five closing questions cover prerequisites, small-utility use, certification boundaries, capstone selection, and safe storage. Answers use direct utility examples and preserve the evidence boundary.

## Recording script

Not required. This is a written-first course and no approved modality requires narration.

## Diversity check

Chapter 00 is an orientation and artifact-setup workshop, unlike Chapter 01's fragmentation diagnosis. Its dominant timeline, release-gate interaction, claim classification, and Launch Pack index are not reused as the Chapter 01 fingerprint. Whole-course distinctiveness must pass.
