| Field | Decision |

| Learner roles | Control owners, data stewards, assurance staff, auditors, leaders |

## Learning job

The learner must refuse to overstate an access-review control, connect the claim to population and evidence, and create a corrective improvement plan.

## Visual pacing review

- A claim ledger creates a documentary opening distinct from adjacent lessons.
- The circular cycle is followed by a two-column evidence review bench.
- Worked access-review numbers make the assurance language concrete.
- Mobile treatment linearizes the loop and evidence bench; reduced motion removes the stamp rotation.
