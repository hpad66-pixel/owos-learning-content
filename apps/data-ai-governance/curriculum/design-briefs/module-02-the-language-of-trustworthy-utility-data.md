# Module Design Brief: Chapter 02

## Identity

Course `owos-master-data-governance-001`; learning object `dga001:02`; **The language of trustworthy utility data**. Controlled anchor is Version 2.3 Chapter 2. Riverbend sample facts and thresholds are instructional.

## Learning job

The learner decides whether drinking-water sample `RB-24-017` can be released, separates core trust terms, writes a minimum provenance event, and states quality for a specific use.

## Concept-to-experience plan

| Idea | Shape | Visual | Action | Result | Quiz |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Origin and responsibility | Network | `provenance-network` | Trace sample to release | Agents, activities, versions, and time become visible | `matching` |
| Fitness for use | Threshold framework | `quality-by-use` | Change intended use | Quality requirement moves with consequence | scenario choice |

## Module design fingerprint

Data-journey laboratory. Signature mechanism is holding the record constant while changing its intended use. Existing term matching, governance comparison, provenance builder, quality model, product stack, artifact builders, and tracker remain. Work product is the governed language starter pack.

## Instructor explanation plan

Explanations separate lineage, provenance, integrity, and traceability, then show why provenance and quality answer different questions. Retry guidance points back to the drinking-water release.

## Written-first review

More than 2,000 teaching words, a complete laboratory worked example, misconception handling, and boundaries remain without media.

## Visual pacing review

Comparison table, provenance network, quality model, product stack, builders, and trackers interrupt prose. Dark surfaces use light text.

## Explanatory graphic plan

The `provenance-network` explains where the result came from. The `quality-by-use` model explains whether the evidence is sufficient for the stated decision. Both have text equivalents and mobile stacking.

## Learner FAQ plan

Five questions cover lineage versus provenance, integrity, quality by use, minimum provenance events, and governed data products.

Not required.
