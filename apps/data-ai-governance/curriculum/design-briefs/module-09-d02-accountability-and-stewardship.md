# Module Design Brief: Chapter 09

## Identity

Course `owos-master-data-governance-001`; learning object `dga001:09`; **D02: Accountability and stewardship**. Controlled anchor is Version 2.3 domain D02. This is the capability benchmark, not a page template.

## Learning job

The learner decides who can authorize treatment of 1,200 conflicting lead-service-line records and builds an operating pack with enforceable authority.

## Concept-to-experience plan

| Idea | Shape | Visual | Action | Result | Quiz |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product accountability | Relationship network | `network-diagram` | Follow evidence to uses | One product supports several decisions | `classify` |
| Decision forum | Gates | `phase-gate` | Frame through closure | Forum supports but does not replace owner | authority check |

## Module design fingerprint

Accountability decision room. Signature mechanism is hearing evidence move through five professional voices and testing authority by role. Existing handoff, relationship map, authority simulator, forum process, supplier map, operating-pack builder, mastery check, and evidence tracker remain.

## Instructor explanation plan

The learner is taught where owner authority starts, what stewardship maintains, what custody operates, how the forum supports decisions, and where assurance must remain independent.

## Written-first review

More than 1,300 visible words after evidence mapping, a lead-service-line worked example, and complete role explanations remain without video.

## Visual pacing review

Decision room, network, ladder, simulator, forum flow, supplier table, builder, and mastery check pace the lesson. Dark surfaces use light text.

## Explanatory graphic plan

The `network-diagram` connects sources, product, and material uses. The `phase-gate` preserves decision authority through the forum. Mobile reduces each to ordered text.

## Learner FAQ plan

Five questions cover ceremonial owners, steward authority, forum limits, separation of duties, and supplier accountability.

## Recording script

Not required.

## Diversity check

This accountability courtroom differs from Chapter 08's investment room and Chapter 10's metadata forensics. Its classify check and operating pack are unique. It remains a capability benchmark only.
