# OWOS Module Design Brief

## Identity

| Field | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Course and module ID | `owos-master-data-governance-001`, `dga001:21` |
| Working title | Thirty findings are not a plan |
| Archetype | Roadmap investment room |
| Controlled sources | Method v2.3 Chapters 6 and 11; syllabus Chapter 21 |

## Learning job

The learner must turn a vague telemetry finding into a bounded immediate control, funded operating change, dependency-aware roadmap, and closure test. The professional work product is an owned and funded governance roadmap.

## Concept-to-experience plan

| Teaching idea | Visual | Learner action | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Funding exposes tradeoffs | `two-by-two` investment board | Change the delivery envelope | `finding-diagnosis` |
| Activity is not capability | `comparison-table` change ledger | Compare transaction with operating proof | `closure-proof-audit` |
| Horizons depend on evidence | `timeline-roadmap` | Select each horizon | Applied roadmap defense |

## Module design fingerprint

- Signature mechanism: sequence findings by dependency, value, risk, and capacity.
- Opening: unsafe telemetry gap competes with a delayed catalog program.
- Narrative: finding, funding tradeoff, transformation test, horizon sequence, closure.
- Purposeful interactions: portfolio funding tradeoff and roadmap horizon sequencing.
- Visual sequence: `two-by-two`, `comparison-table`, `timeline-roadmap`.
- Quiz sequence: `finding-diagnosis`, `closure-proof-audit`, final applied defense.
- Work-product mode: funded governance roadmap.
- Role lenses: foundation, practitioner, leader.
- FAQ: six investment-room questions.
- Drawers and bottom connected learning: governed shared components.

## Instructor explanation plan

The investment board explanation tells the learner how constraints change funded, staged, and waiting actions. The change-ledger explanation separates completed deliverables from recurring operating evidence. The horizon explanation traces protection, mobilization, operation, integration, and conditional scale. Each assessment explanation states the evidence being judged.

## Visual pacing review

No more than two prose blocks occur without a worked example, callout, visual, assessment, role view, or work product. Dark surfaces use explicit shared light-text rules. The mobile view stacks comparison panels and preserves horizontal roadmap labels. Reduced motion removes transitions without removing state.

## Explanatory graphic plan

| Graphic | Learner conclusion | Accessible treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Investment board | Critical controls and enabling dependencies precede attractive isolated purchases | Buttons, live text, card status |
| Change ledger | A purchase enables capability but does not prove it | Semantic paired lists |
| Roadmap band | Each horizon requires evidence before the next receives funds | Labeled buttons and live explanation |

## Learner FAQ plan

Questions address maturity-gap priority, unknown root cause, risk acceptance, estimate precision, small-utility adaptation, and closure. Answers use the Riverbend roadmap and retain local authority boundaries.

## Recording script

Not planned. The lesson is written-first and complete without video.

## Diversity check

Chapter 20 is assessment fieldwork. Chapter 21 changes to an investment room, funding-envelope mechanism, transaction-versus-transformation comparison, and roadmap artifact. Chapter 22 changes again to a week-by-week mobilization. The interaction signature and quiz sequence are unique.
