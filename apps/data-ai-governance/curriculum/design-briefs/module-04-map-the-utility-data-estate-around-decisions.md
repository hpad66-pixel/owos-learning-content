# Module Design Brief: Chapter 04

## Identity

Course `owos-master-data-governance-001`; learning object `dga001:04`; **Map the utility data estate around decisions**. Controlled anchor is Version 2.3 Chapter 4. Riverbend systems and scores are instructional.

## Learning job

The learner selects priority capital-renewal decisions and maps physical, digital, human, supplier, model, authority, and outcome relationships.

## Concept-to-experience plan

| Idea | Shape | Visual | Action | Result | Quiz |

## Module design fingerprint

Map-based exploration. Signature mechanism is filtering Riverbend's estate around a capital-renewal question. Existing estate map, sorter, domain tree, handoff, priority grid, shortlist, builder, and tracker remain. Work product is the decision-centered estate map.

## Instructor explanation plan

The map is read from physical reality outward. The priority view explains why high-consequence, highly fragmented decisions are mapped first.

## Written-first review

More than 1,200 words and a pump-renewal worked example remain complete without video.

## Visual pacing review

Estate map, domain tree, handoff simulator, priority grid, shortlist, and builder break prose. Dark surfaces use light text.

## Explanatory graphic plan

The `estate-map` connects the asset to evidence and authority. The `two-by-two` governs scope priority. Both collapse to one-column text order on mobile.

## Learner FAQ plan

Five questions cover application inventories, decision-first scope, initial decision count, unknown interfaces, and relationship to existing inventories.

Not required.
