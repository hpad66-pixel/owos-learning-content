# Module Design Brief: Chapter 08

## Identity

Course `owos-master-data-governance-001`; learning object `dga001:08`; **D01: Strategy, value, and decision rights**. Controlled anchor is Version 2.3 domain D01.

## Learning job

The learner decides which governance initiative to fund, defines the stopping gate, and builds a board-ready strategy pack.

## Concept-to-experience plan

| Idea | Shape | Visual | Action | Result | Quiz |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Whole governance scope | Hierarchy | `layered-stack` | Open scope layers | IT, OT, AI, suppliers, and lifecycle connect | `ordering` |
| Governed portfolio | Status matrix | `heat-grid` | Rebalance after evidence failure | Funding changes with proof and risk | investment check |

## Module design fingerprint

Executive investment room. Signature mechanism is portfolio rebalancing after a failed evidence gate. Existing scope stack, value tree, risk grid, decision exercise, scoreboard, roadmap, builders, and tracker remain. Work product is the D01 strategy pack.

## Instructor explanation plan

The lesson starts from utility outcome and material decision, then adds appetite, authority, evidence gate, capacity, and funding. Technology-first options are explicit non-examples.

## Written-first review

More than 1,100 words and a pump-prediction investment example remain without video.

## Visual pacing review

Scope stack, value tree, heat grid, scoreboard, roadmap, builder, and tracker pace the lesson. Dark surfaces use light text.

## Explanatory graphic plan

The `layered-stack` proves the charter covers the whole decision system. The `heat-grid` makes funding, condition, pause, and stop decisions visible.

## Learner FAQ plan

Five questions cover charters, value evidence, risk appetite, critical gates, and roadmap change.

## Recording script

Not required.

## Diversity check

This investment room differs from Chapter 07's role conference and Chapter 09's accountability decision room. Its ordering check and board pack are unique.

## Approval

Executive, governing-body, factual, accessibility, and release reviews remain pending.
