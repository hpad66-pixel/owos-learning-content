Shift decision

May the optimization agent start P-204?

The twin says “available.” The work-management system says the pump is active. A local lockout tag and operator log say it is isolated for seal replacement. The optimization agent proposes a start command to satisfy a pressure target.

1 | Separate the states

One pump can have several valid descriptions at once

Operational technology, or OT, includes field instruments, controllers, supervisory systems, treatment processes, pumps, valves, and communications that affect real operations. The Enterprise Asset Management record might call P-204 “active” because it is installed. Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition might show “stopped” because the motor is not running. The work permit might say “isolated.” A hydraulic model might estimate “available” because its last synchronized configuration did not include the isolation.

A digital twin is not merely a drawing or a live dashboard. It connects identities, structure, observations, behavior, rules, models, and time for a defined use. The words “live” and “real time” do not guarantee correctness. A value can be recent but attached to the wrong asset, wrong unit, wrong clock, or stale configuration.

Read this stack from the plant upward, but do not assume the top layer is superior. Physical state exists whether software sees it or not. Observed state is produced by instruments and communications. Model state is calculated from assumptions and inputs. Authority state determines who or what may act. A safe decision makes the differences explicit.

Physical stateP-204 is electrically and mechanically isolated.
Observed stateMotor current is zero; local switch status is unavailable.
Model stateTwin estimates available from yesterday's configuration.
Authority stateOnly the operator may clear isolation after the permit closes.

Ask what each state represents, how it was produced, when it was valid, and whether it carries authority to act.

A disagreement is not noise to hide. It is evidence that identity, time, synchronization, or authority needs attention.

Worked control-room reconstruction

Follow P-204 across four systems

Operator Sam starts with the physical identifier on the equipment and verifies the lockout tag. The OT engineer checks the controller point and finds that a maintenance override prevented the isolation status from reaching the supervisory system. The asset steward confirms that “active” means installed, not available. The twin engineer discovers that the nightly configuration extract ran before the work permit opened.

The team does not pick a single “source of truth” for every question. It declares the authoritative source by property and time. The asset register controls installed identity and class. The permit system controls isolation state. SCADA provides timestamped observations. The twin estimates hydraulic consequences. The operating procedure and shift authority control whether a command may be executed. Riverbend fixes the mapping, adds the permit state to the twin context, records synchronization health, and prevents recommendations involving isolated assets.

2 | Connect identity before telemetry

A tag becomes trustworthy only when its relationships are known

Asset identity must bridge engineering drawings, EAM, geographic information systems, SCADA tags, permits, inspection records, and models. Name matching is not enough. “P204,” “P-204,” and “North Pump 2” may refer to the same asset, while a replacement motor may inherit a location but not the historical identity of the old equipment.

Use the network as a relationship checklist. The center is the governed asset identity. Every connected system contributes a different property or event. Select the system whose evidence answers the question being asked. If a connection relies only on text similarity or manual memory, mark it for identity reconciliation before using it for automation.

EAMInstalled identity, class, parent, lifecycle
SCADATag, observation, quality, timestamp
Permit systemIsolation, responsible person, release
GISLocation and network connectivity
Twin modelModel object, version, assumptions
ProcedureOperating limit and authority

Trace from the governed asset to each property source. Do not collapse observation, administrative status, model estimate, and permission.

A useful twin is a governed assembly of relationships, not a replacement for the systems and people that produce evidence.

3 | Test the twin before it influences action

Run the disagreement console

Synchronization lag should be observable. So should missing inputs, sensor quality, model version, confidence, and operating limits. A control boundary distinguishes advisory insight from supervised action and autonomous command.

Select each incident and watch which state fails. The console does not ask whether the twin is generally accurate. It asks whether the specific evidence path is current, identified, valid for the property, and authorized for the proposed action. The safest response can be continue, degrade to advisory mode, or stop.

Compare freshness, evidence quality, model confidence, and command authority. Confidence is only one signal.

The twin may remain useful in a degraded advisory role even when it must not control equipment.

Authority check

Put the evidence in the right order

This check tests operational sequencing. When a recommendation conflicts with a field isolation, first protect the physical process, then verify authority and identity, then reconcile observations and the model. Choose the sequence that preserves safety and produces evidence instead of silently forcing agreement.

Questions from the control room

OT and digital twin FAQ

Is a live dashboard a digital twin?

Not necessarily. A dashboard can display observations. A governed twin also connects identities, structure, behavior, time, model versions, assumptions, purpose, and operating limits.

Which system is the source of truth?

Name authority by property and time. EAM may govern installed identity, a permit system isolation, SCADA observation, and an operator the right to act. One universal source is often the wrong model.

Can a twin control equipment directly?

Only within an explicitly approved control boundary supported by hazard analysis, cybersecurity, testing, monitoring, fail-safe behavior, human authority, change control, and recovery evidence.

What happens when telemetry is missing?

The twin should expose uncertainty and enter a defined degraded mode. It may continue an advisory calculation, use a bounded fallback, or stop, depending on consequence and approved rules.

How do replacements affect asset identity?

Separate the physical item, functional position, tag, model object, and history. A new motor can occupy an old position without becoming the same historical asset.

Who owns a digital twin?

Ownership is shared by responsibility: operations owns the use and consequence, asset and OT teams control evidence, model owners control assumptions and versions, and governance defines accountability and review.

Evidence boundary. P-204 and its signals are instructional. This lesson does not authorize a command, establish a safety case, define a cybersecurity architecture, or certify a digital twin. Use current plant procedures, control-system engineering, safety, cybersecurity, vendor, regulatory, and operator review. Reference points include the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, the CISA Industrial Control Systems resources, and the ISO 55000 asset management overview.
