1 | Define the product

The product is a service around a decision

A data product has a user, decision, outcome, accountable product owner, and measurable service promise. Its data contract tells producers and consumers what can be relied on. A read-only connector can preserve source authority while exposing a bounded view.

The shared application spine connects asset, observation, event, work, rule, evidence, decision, and outcome records. Service level describes what the product will provide. Rollback describes what happens when the release harms a decision or cannot meet its contract.

Separate ownership jobs. The product owner is accountable for user value and contract. The platform owner operates shared technology. Source owners remain accountable for authoritative records. Decision owners retain business or operational authority.
Worked Riverbend product

Network risk for capital planning, not an unexplained score

Riverbend defines the user as the capital planning team and the decision as prioritizing candidate main renewals for engineering review. The product does not approve projects automatically. It connects stable asset identity to break history, inspection, hydraulic criticality, customer and service consequence, current cost range, data quality state, model version, and the approved capital decision.

The contract states daily refresh for work history, quarterly model baselines, known gaps for legacy pipe material, access limits for customer-sensitive consequence data, and a correction path through asset stewardship. A source-to-output lineage record identifies every transformation. Adoption is measured by decisions using the product; usefulness is measured by better review time, visible exceptions, fewer identity disputes, and outcomes, not by query count alone.

Product contract anatomy

Build from purpose down to lifecycle

Read this stack from the decision at the top to retirement at the bottom. Every layer answers a different consumer question. A product can have excellent technology and still fail because its purpose, authority, rights, support, or correction path is missing. At each layer, ask which role supplies the evidence, which consumer relies on it, and what happens when the promise cannot be met.

Purpose User, decision, outcome, consequence, authority
Meaning Identity, schema, units, semantics, valid time
Evidence Sources, lineage, provenance, quality, limitations
Service Access, freshness, availability, support, change notice
Control Rights, security, retention, incidents, correction, rollback
Lifecycle Adoption, outcome, review, version, deprecation, retirement

Ask whether a named consumer can understand the promise, verify the evidence, handle an exception, and know when the product changes.

A product contract makes reuse safe by connecting technical delivery to accountable service and decision limits.

Product classification

Which object is actually a governed data product?

Choose the option with a named user and accountable service. A file, table, dashboard, or API may be part of a product. It becomes a governed product only when purpose, meaning, sources, quality, rights, service, support, change, and lifecycle are operated.

2 | Reuse the shared spine

One asset should not become four unrelated assets

Pump OS, Pipe OS, Reg OS, and future agentic applications should not invent separate identities and meanings for the same pump, pipe, sample, work order, permit rule, or approved decision. The spine supplies shared identifiers, governed concepts, relationships, source references, and evidence states. Each application adds its own workflow and user experience without rewriting enterprise truth.

Select an application and inspect the records it needs. The center remains shared while the outer workflow changes. Notice that applications consume only the records and permissions required for their decision. Reuse does not mean giving every application unrestricted access to everything. Compare the AI agent view with the operating applications and identify where policy, provenance, and human action authority must travel with retrieved information.

Read the shared row first, then the application-specific records. The connector carries governed references and limits.

A shared spine reduces duplicated meaning while preserving bounded consumer views and decision authority.

Release evidence

What must the read-only connector carry?

Select the complete connector packet. A read-only interface reduces write risk, but it does not solve identity, meaning, rights, lineage, freshness, quality, or consumer limitations. Those travel with the connection.

3 | Gate the release

Useful enough is an evidence decision

Toggle each release gate for the Riverbend product. A failed critical gate keeps the product in review even when the prototype is attractive. The board makes the missing proof visible and shows what can be tested, bounded, corrected, or rolled back. Do not count green gates and average them against a critical failure. Read the verdict, name the accountable reviewer, and state the exact evidence needed to change a hold.

Inspect purpose, data, control, authority, outcome, and recovery. Critical failures cannot be averaged into a passing score.

A release is defensible when the utility can state what the product supports, what it does not, who decides, and how harm is contained and corrected.

Usefulness judgment

When should the product expand to another application?

Choose the expansion decision that relies on operating evidence. Reuse is valuable only when the shared contract still fits the new user and decision. A successful dashboard does not automatically justify operational control or a higher-consequence use.

Final applied check

Questions from product teams

Data product and application spine FAQ

Is an API a data product?

It can be a delivery interface for one. The governed product also needs purpose, user, owner, meaning, sources, quality, rights, lineage, service, support, correction, and lifecycle.

Does the shared spine require copying all data?

No. It can use governed references, virtual mappings, APIs, indexes, or materialized records according to latency, cost, source authority, security, and evidence needs.

Who owns the product when several systems contribute?

Name one accountable product owner. Source owners remain accountable for their records, platform owners operate technology, and decision owners retain authority for the use.

Can an AI agent query the spine?

Yes, through governed, permission-aware interfaces that return source, time, meaning, quality, rights, lineage, limits, and permitted actions. The graph does not give the agent unlimited authority.

What is the difference between adoption and usefulness?

Adoption asks whether intended users use the product. Usefulness asks whether it improves the stated decision or service outcome without unacceptable harm, cost, or control failure.

When should a data product be retired?

When its decision disappears, authority changes, sources or controls cannot be sustained, a successor is approved, or evidence shows the product no longer delivers justified value. Plan migration and retained evidence.

Evidence boundary. Riverbend and the network-risk product are instructional. This lesson does not approve architecture, access, capital decisions, operational control, supplier terms, or production release. Verify actual source authority, security, privacy, records, model, engineering, support, and decision requirements. Reference points include the controlled course syllabus, the W3C PROV-O Recommendation, and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework.
