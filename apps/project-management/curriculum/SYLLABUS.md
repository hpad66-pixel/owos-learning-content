# PART B — Scope, Requirements & Schedule
*KA: Scope · Schedule · PMBOK7: Planning*

- **B1 · Requirements management** ★NEW · Core · eliciting & validating requirements; design criteria, hydraulic & regulatory requirements; traceability.
- **B2 · Scope & the Work Breakdown Structure** · Core · scope statement, WBS, work packages, acceptance criteria, scope creep, definition of done.
- **B3 · Schedule fundamentals & the Gantt** · Core · activities, durations, dependencies (FS/SS/FF/SF), milestones, baseline. ◆
- **B4 · Sequencing & network diagrams** ★NEW · Core · Activity-on-Node / Precedence Diagramming, leads & lags, logic.

- **B8 · Schedule risk & Monte Carlo** ★NEW · Deep-dive [WoW] · probabilistic schedule, P50/P80 dates, confidence.
- **B9 · Loading the schedule — cost & resource loading** ★NEW · Core · cost-loaded schedule &rarr; cash-flow S-curve (draw schedule, EVM baseline); resource-loaded schedule &rarr; crew/equipment histogram, over-allocation, leveling vs smoothing. *(Built interactively into the B3+B6 Studio: Dependencies / Cost-loaded / Resource-loaded tabs.)*
