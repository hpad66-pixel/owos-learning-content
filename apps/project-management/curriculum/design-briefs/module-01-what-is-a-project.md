## Learner FAQ plan

| Likely learner question | Why it may remain unclear | Direct plain-English answer | Utility example | Diagram, comparison, or worked sequence | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Can recurring work ever be managed as a project? | The lesson can be misapplied without the boundary | Yes. A one-time improvement to recurring work can be a project because the improvement has a finish line. The routine that remains afterward is operations. | Millpond or utility delivery context | Direct comparison or worked interpretation | Controlled local evidence governs |
| Does a positive NPV automatically authorize the work? | The lesson can be misapplied without the boundary | No. It supports the value case, but authorization also depends on risk, affordability, legal authority, capacity, and competing priorities. | Millpond or utility delivery context | Direct comparison or worked interpretation | Controlled local evidence governs |
| Why is quality shown inside the constraint model? | The lesson can be misapplied without the boundary | Quality reflects how well the promised scope is delivered within the time and cost commitments. If those commitments conflict, quality and risk usually absorb the pressure. | Millpond or utility delivery context | Direct comparison or worked interpretation | Controlled local evidence governs |
| What if the benefit is public health rather than cash savings? | The lesson can be misapplied without the boundary | State the benefit and the evidence honestly. A utility business case can include avoided risk and service outcomes, but those should not be disguised as precise cash when the evidence does not support it. | Millpond or utility delivery context | Direct comparison or worked interpretation | Controlled local evidence governs |
| Who owns the assumptions in the business case? | The lesson can be misapplied without the boundary | The sponsor owns the decision, while named technical, financial, operational, and project roles should own the assumptions they are qualified to support. | Millpond or utility delivery context | Direct comparison or worked interpretation | Controlled local evidence governs |
