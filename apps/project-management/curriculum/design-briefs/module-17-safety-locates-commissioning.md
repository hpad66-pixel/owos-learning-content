## Written-first review

- Approximate conversational teaching words: More than 2,000.
- Worked utility example and marker: Eight-foot Millpond trench, marked gas crossing, and incomplete laboratory clearance.
- Misconception addressed: Valid ticket, passing first sample, or high percent complete is enough to proceed.
- Boundary or non-example: The lesson never issues a site authorization.
- Component debriefs: Present after hierarchy, locate, clearance, readiness board, and evidence tracker.
- What remains if every video and animation is removed: Full field explanation, gate facts, work product, FAQ, evidence boundary, and completion rules.

## Visual pacing review
