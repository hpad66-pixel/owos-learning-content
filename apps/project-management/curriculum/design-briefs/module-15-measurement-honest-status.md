## Learning job

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| What consequential situation opens the lesson? | The board packet says green while current cost and schedule evidence indicates action is needed. |
| What must the learner decide before teaching begins? | Accept the summary or challenge and reconstruct it from evidence. |
| What professional consequence makes this matter? | A false green removes the time and authority needed to recover the project. |
| What should the learner be able to do afterward? | Trace rating to current evidence, trend, consequence, response, and decision. |
| What usable work product will the learner create? | Decision-ready executive status brief. |
| What evidence is required for completion? | Opening decision, dashboard forensics, completed brief, final status gate. |

## Written-first review

- Approximate conversational teaching words: More than 1,700.
- Worked utility example and marker: Millpond green board packet with cost-performance index 0.87 and eroding float.
- Misconception addressed: Most-green measures justify an overall green.
- Boundary or non-example: Millpond thresholds are instructional, not universal governance rules.
- Component debriefs: Present after dashboard, forensics, comparison, and artifact.
- What remains if every video and animation is removed: Complete measure teaching, evidence cases, work product, FAQ, sources, and completion controls.
