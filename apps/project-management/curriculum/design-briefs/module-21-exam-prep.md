| Question | Answer |

## Written-first review

- Approximate teaching words: More than 4,000.
- Worked example: Mixed-domain practice set and earned-value question.
- Misconception: This course determines eligibility or one high score proves readiness.
- Boundary: Requirements change and must be verified.
- Debriefs: Present.
- Without media: Complete study method, practice, plan, FAQ, and boundary.

## Visual pacing review

- Longest prose run: Two.
- Breaks: Comparison tables, classifications, formula check, practice runner, loop, gate, builder.
- Exception: None.
- Illustration: Not applicable.
- Contrast: Explicit light text on dark surfaces.
