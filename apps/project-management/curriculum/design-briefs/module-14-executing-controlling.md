## Learning job

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Consequential situation | Millpond is active, reports are changing, and a field reroute is proposed. |
| Initial decision | Whether work may proceed before integrated effects and authority are known. |
| Professional consequence | Unofficial change creates drift, cost exposure, delay, rework, and dispute. |
| Post-lesson capability | Connect request, integrated analysis, authority, baselines, communication, and verification. |
| Work product | Change decision record. |
| Completion evidence | Opening, change gate, record, and final record check. |
