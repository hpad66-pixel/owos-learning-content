| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Consequential situation | A lower bid may cost more to own and operations may receive an unusable handover. |

| Professional consequence | Poor choice or handover creates decades of cost, downtime, unsafe operation, and lost knowledge. |
| Post-lesson capability | Connect lifecycle basis, identity, acceptance, operating readiness, and resilience. |
| Work product | Operating-handover brief. |
| Completion evidence | Opening, handover gate, brief, final check. |

## Written-first review

- Approximate teaching words: More than 3,000.
- Worked example: Wellfield pump lifecycle and firm-capacity loss.
- Misconception: Lowest bid is lowest cost, or record drawings equal handover.
- Boundary: Instructional values do not recommend a live option.
- Debriefs: Present.
- Without media: Complete instruction, comparison, artifact, FAQ, and boundary.

## Visual pacing review

- Longest prose run: Two.
- Pacing breaks: Cost model, system sorter, capacity model, network, gate, brief.
- Exception: None.
- Illustration: Not applicable.
- Contrast: Explicit light text on dark surfaces.
