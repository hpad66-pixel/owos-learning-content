# Module Design Brief: Module 10 Quality Management

## Identity

| Field | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Course and module ID | `owos-master-project-delivery-001` / `pm-10-quality-management` |
| Working title | Quality: building it right, not gold-plating it |
| Learner roles | Utility project manager, inspector, construction manager, engineer, operator, sponsor |
| Competencies | Distinguish assurance from control, place hold points, diagnose non-conformance, verify repair |
| Controlled sources | ISO quality-management overview; Federal Highway Administration construction-quality guidance; project-specific contract documents govern real work |

## Learning job

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| What consequential situation opens the lesson? | Millpond work is about to be buried and an observed defect may indicate a process failure. |
| What must the learner decide before teaching begins? | Whether the condition can proceed or must stop for controlled diagnosis and disposition. |
| What professional consequence makes this matter? | A visible low-cost correction can become buried failure, rework, dispute, or operating risk. |
| What should the learner be able to do afterward? | Connect requirement, evidence, authority, correction, verification, and closure. |
| What usable work product will the learner create? | Quality repair record. |
| What evidence is required for completion? | Opening condition, quality-clinic assessment, completed repair record, final quality-record gate. |

## Written-first review

- Approximate conversational teaching words: More than 1,500.
- Worked utility example and marker: Millpond repeated gasket defects at the pre-backfill hold point.
- Misconception addressed: An NCR is punishment, or physical repair alone closes the issue.
- Boundary or non-example: A real disposition follows contract authority and approved technical requirements.
- Component debriefs: Visible after both governed visuals and the clinic.
- What remains if every video and animation is removed: Complete explanation, worked cases, repair sequence, work product, feedback, FAQ, evidence, and completion controls.

## Visual pacing review

- Longest run of consecutive full prose blocks: Two.
- Visual, interaction, worked example, or callout used to break each dense section: Phase gate, curve, repair ordering, clinic, and builder.
- Any uninterrupted prose exception and reason: None.
- Editorial illustration reading guide and learner conclusion, when used: Not applicable.
- Dark-surface contrast plan: Drawer header explicitly uses white text on brand blue.

## Learner FAQ plan

| Likely learner question | Why it may remain unclear | Direct plain-English answer | Utility example | Diagram, comparison, or worked sequence | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Does NCR mean fault? | Record and liability are confused | It records non-conformance; responsibility may require separate review | Wrong valve may trace to drawing, supplier, or installation | Requirement-to-evidence chain | Contract governs |
| Can use-as-is be approved? | Authority is assumed | Only an authorized role with sufficient evidence can approve | Valve operating convention | Disposition comparison | Local authority governs |
| Why not repair first? | Speed hides evidence loss | Preserve the condition before change | Photograph and locate gasket defect | Repair sequence | Controlled record governs |
| Are many NCRs bad? | Count lacks context | Review severity, recurrence, age, and closure | Ten minor items versus one hidden repeated defect | Comparison | No assurance claim |
| Where should hold points sit? | Teams add checkpoints by habit | Put them before hidden or irreversible work | Joint inspection before backfill | Phase gate | Project plan governs |
