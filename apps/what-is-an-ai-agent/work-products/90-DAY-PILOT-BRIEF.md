## Days 1 through 30: Establish the baseline

- Confirm current workflow and measures.
- Preserve historical test cases.
- Verify sources, ownership, identity, and permissions.
- Define allowed, approval-required, and prohibited actions.
- Build evaluation and failure cases before broad use.

## Days 31 through 60: Run in observation or recommendation mode

- Use read-only or recommendation-only permissions.
- Compare agent results with historical outcomes and human work.
- Record unsupported claims, tool failures, retries, cost, delay, and review effort.
- Hold weekly practitioner and security review.

## Days 61 through 90: Decide whether to expand

- Review outcome measures and failure patterns.
- Recheck data, permissions, guardrails, and owner accountability.
- Decide whether to expand, simplify, redesign, pause, or stop.
- Expand permissions only when evidence supports the change.

## Stop conditions

- Unauthorized action
- Material privacy or security concern
- Repeated unsupported output above the approved threshold
- Missing or unavailable accountable owner
- Cost or retry threshold exceeded
- Tool or source behavior outside the tested boundary
- Operational-technology impact outside the approved pilot
