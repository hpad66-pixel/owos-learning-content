# Bottom Connected Learning and Module FAQs

Date: 2026-07-23

## Hardeep Anand direction

- The complete Graph and Community cards must not hang from the top of the course or cover the lesson.
- The top of the course should contain only compact buttons that tell learners Graph and Community are available.
- Graph and Community must open from the side in white space, then close and return the learner to the same place.
- The complete connected-learning experience must remain at the bottom, consistent with the other courses.
- Every current and future module must include an FAQ at the bottom.
- FAQs should anticipate likely student questions and answer them proactively in simple English.
- Use explanations, examples, diagrams, comparisons, and worked sequences where they help the learner understand.

## Implementation decision

- Anchor the complete shared connected-learning section to the course `main` element immediately before bottom navigation.
- Keep compact Graph and Community header buttons and responsive side drawers.
- Add four distinct FAQs to each AI Agent module, with a visual module map, direct answers, utility examples, semantic disclosure controls, and a path to Community.
- Bind the FAQ requirement into syllabus preparation, module design, writing, QA, course scaffolding, automated testing, and release controls.
