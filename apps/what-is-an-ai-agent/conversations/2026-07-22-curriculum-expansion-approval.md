# Curriculum Expansion Approval

Date: 2026-07-22
Approved by: Hardeep Anand

Hardeep approved adding the complete novice-learning framework to the foundational AI agent course. The curriculum will include recognition, mechanism, agent anatomy, utility relevance, agenticness and autonomy, data and knowledge readiness, orchestration and handoffs, guardrails and human authority, failure literacy, value and feasibility, workforce implications, and personal application.

The course will include a progressive Utility Agent Canvas and 90-Day Pilot Brief capstone. Learners will complete one portion during each module rather than receiving a blank capstone at the end.

All technical terms must be introduced in plain English. Terms and acronyms will use accessible tooltips throughout the lesson experience. The anatomy of an agent will be taught as an interactive system whose parts can be opened, tested, and connected to utility work.
