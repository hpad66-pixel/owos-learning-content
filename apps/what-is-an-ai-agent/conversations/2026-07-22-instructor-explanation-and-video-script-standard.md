# Instructor Explanation and Video Script Direction

Date: 2026-07-22

Hardeep directed that every visual, animation, simulation, quiz, and work product must be taught in simple English. The learner must not be expected to infer the point from the interface. Each major component needs one or two instructor paragraphs that explain what the learner is seeing, what action to take, what to notice, why it matters in utility work, and what the result means.

Hardeep also directed that every module must have a recording script. The course needs one overview video script that explains lessons 1 through 8 in order. Scripts must be written for speaking, separate visual cues from spoken words, define technical terms in ordinary language, and connect each lesson to water, wastewater, or stormwater work.

This direction applies to Module 4 now and becomes a durable requirement for every future OWOS course lesson.
