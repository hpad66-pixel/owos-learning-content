# Foundational AI Agent Course Direction

Date: 2026-07-22
Contributor: Hardeep Anand
Status: approved course direction for research and blueprint development

## Hardeep's direction

Use the uploaded McKinsey document as one source for understanding what an AI agent is. Build a foundational course for professionals working in water, wastewater, and stormwater utilities.

This course should focus on what an agent is. Separate future courses will address retrieval-augmented generation, chatbots, and other artificial-intelligence terms in greater depth. This course may introduce prerequisite concepts and adjacent terms only when they help the learner understand agents.

The learner should experience clear aha moments that reveal how an agent actually works. The course should use purposeful animations, simulations, dynamic engagement, streaming behavior, orchestration, agent types, handoffs, and guardrails. It should explain how these ideas connect to utility applications and help learners imagine how utilities may become more agentic.

The course must clearly distinguish an agent from agentic behavior or an agentic system. Hardeep believes these are related but different concepts and wants the distinction tested and clarified through evidence.

## Intended result

The learner should leave with a strong foundational understanding of agents, their operating parts, their limitations, their handoffs, and credible utility use cases. The experience should be visually varied, interactive, professionally useful, and grounded in controlled sources.
