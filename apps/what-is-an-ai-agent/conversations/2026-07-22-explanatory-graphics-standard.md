# Explanatory Graphics Direction

Date: 2026-07-22

Hardeep directed that the plain-English instructor standard apply to every OWOS course and module. HTML components without explanation are not instruction.

Hardeep also directed that graphics be used where they accentuate, exemplify, or amplify a concept, method, framework, relationship, or consequence. Graphics must not be cheesy or decorative. Each graphic needs a clear instructional purpose, an explanation of how to read it, and a conclusion the learner can state.

For Module 4, the approved next-version direction is to add a visual anatomy of the controlled handoff packet and a cause map that shows how one missing evidence locator affects downstream roles.
