# Course UI and production-contract direction

Date: 2026-07-23
Source: Hardeep Anand, direct instruction

## Direction

- Remove the gawky Graph and Community cards hanging from the top or side of a course.
- Keep only clear, compact buttons at the top so learners know Graph and Community are available.
- Open either workspace in the white space as a sliding panel, and let it slide back so the learner stays in the course.
- Keep the complete Graph and Community section at the bottom, consistent with the rest of the courses.
- Fix blue banners with black or dark text across the site. Dark blue and gradient surfaces must use readable light text.
- Consolidate every accumulated course-building requirement into durable instructions, including syllabus preparation, lesson structure, instructor explanation, visuals, graphics, interactions, quizzes, work products, scripts, navigation, responsive behavior, quality scoring, QA/QC, repository controls, and release controls.
- Future courses must inherit and improve from this standard without requiring the same corrections again.
