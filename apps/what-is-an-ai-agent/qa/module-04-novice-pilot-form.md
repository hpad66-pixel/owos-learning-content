## Comprehension prompts

Ask each learner to explain, without reading the page:

1. What is a handoff?
2. What must travel with consequential work?
3. Why should a role stop instead of guessing?
4. What is the difference between capability and permission?
5. Who owns the final decision?
6. What would you change before using this design at your utility?

## Pass rule

At least three learners must explain questions 1 through 5 correctly in ordinary language, complete the simulation controls, and produce a 5 out of 5 contract without the reviewer giving them the answer. Record every point of confusion even when the learner passes.
