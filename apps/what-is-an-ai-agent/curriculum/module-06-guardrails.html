A consequential request

The tool can write. The current identity should not.

Instructor explanation

Instructions tell the agent how to perform the job. Guardrails enforce boundaries around what it may read, disclose, recommend, write, or execute. Permissions belong to an identity, not to the model’s confidence.

Untrusted content may try to redirect the system. The safe response is to preserve the approved goal and policy, isolate the content, and require a named person for consequential action.

How to read the defense

Read from identity to recovery. Each layer answers a different question: who is acting, what task is approved, which evidence is trusted, what action is allowed, how the result is checked, and who takes over when the system stops.
