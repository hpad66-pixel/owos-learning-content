# Assessment Blueprint

## Assessment philosophy

The course evaluates recognition, explanation, diagnosis, design, and professional judgment. Watching a page or scrolling to the bottom is not evidence of learning.

## Module evidence

| Module | Decision evidence | Interaction evidence | Knowledge evidence | Work-product evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Correctly classify the proposed system | Capability builder completed | Terms and distinctions explained | Problem and current process saved |
| 2 | Select stop or escalation at the right point | Loop and event stream completed | Sequence and event interpretation passed | Agent Loop Trace saved |
| 3 | Diagnose missing functional parts | Anatomy and permission repairs completed | Dependencies and consequences explained | Readiness Map saved |
| 4 | Select and repair an orchestration design | Handoff playback and failure repair completed | Roles, evidence, and authority passed | Handoff Contract saved |
| 5 | Select the least complicated architecture | Autonomy and consequence placement completed | Agenticness distinction explained | Decision Record saved |
| 6 | Configure bounded permissions and human control | Failure lab and permission console completed | Excessive agency and guardrails passed | Guardrail Plan saved |
| 7 | Advance, defer, simplify, or reject proposals | Portfolio prioritizer completed | Value, readiness, and consequence passed | Opportunity Portfolio saved |
| 8 | Defend a bounded pilot | Canvas builder and adversarial review completed | Evaluation and architecture defense passed | Canvas and Pilot Brief completed |

## Deterministic scoring

- Classification, ordering, matching, permission, and multi-select items use explicit answer keys.
- Every scored response provides an explanation.
- Scenario decisions may allow more than one defensible answer only when the scoring contract names the acceptable conditions.
- Reflection items are not automatically marked correct. They produce a model answer, review checklist, or instructor-review request.
- Capstone completion is based on required fields and review evidence, not a model's unsupported opinion.

## Evaluation categories

| Category | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Concept recognition and explanation | 20% |
| Mechanism and handoff diagnosis | 20% |
| Guardrails, authority, and failure response | 25% |
| Utility value and architecture judgment | 15% |
| Capstone completeness and defensibility | 20% |

## Completion standard

- Complete every required simulation.
- Earn at least 80 percent on deterministic knowledge checks, with unlimited review and retry.
- Save all eight progressive capstone sections.
- Resolve or explicitly accept each adversarial-review finding.
- Submit the Utility Agent Canvas and 90-Day Pilot Brief.
- Explain why the proposed use case needs an agent or why a simpler architecture should be used.

## Credential boundary

Course completion demonstrates foundational AI-agent fluency and completion of a governed pilot design. It does not certify engineering competence, cybersecurity approval, operational authorization, regulatory compliance, or production readiness.
