Flow, QiInfluent flow. The wastewater volume entering the plant. Storms drive it up and overload the clarifier.Design: ~20,000 m³/d—
BODiBiochemical oxygen demand. The organic food load for the bugs. Sets oxygen demand and sludge production.Typical: 150–220 mg/L—
TSSiTotal suspended solids in. Adds inert solids to the mixed liquor inventory.Typical: 180–240 mg/L—
Ammonia, NH₃iAmmonia-nitrogen load. Nitrifiers convert it to nitrate. They are slow-growing and temperature-sensitive.Typical: 25–45 mg/L—
TempiWastewater temperature. Cold water slows the bugs — especially nitrifiers — so you need more sludge age in winter.Warm ~18°C · Cold ~9°C—

Aeration Basin the reactor

MLSSiMixed liquor suspended solids. The concentration of biomass in the tank. You raise it by wasting less, lower it by wasting more.Target: 2,000–3,500 mg/L—
SRTiSolids retention time (sludge age). Days a solids particle stays in the system. The master control variable — set by your wasting rate.Target: 8–15 d (higher in cold)—
F:MiFood-to-microorganism ratio. Load divided by biomass. Too high starves oxygen; too low makes old pin-floc.Conventional: 0.2–0.5 /d—
Dissolved O₂iDO setpoint. Oxygen for the bugs. Below ~2 mg/L nitrification stalls and low-DO filaments bulk the sludge. Higher DO costs energy.Target: 1.8–2.5 mg/L—
MLVSSiVolatile (living) fraction of MLSS. The active biomass, roughly 78% of MLSS here.~0.78 × MLSS—

Secondary Clarifier solids/liquid split

SVIiSludge volume index. How well the sludge settles. Rising SVI = bulking = the clarifier loses capacity.Good <120 · Bulking >180 mL/g—
BlanketiSludge blanket depth. How high settled solids stack in the clarifier. If it reaches the weir, solids wash out into the effluent.Safe <1 m · Danger >3 m (4 m = weir)—
RAS conc.iReturn sludge concentration. How thick the underflow is. Bulking sludge won't thicken, so wasting removes less mass.Typically 2–3 × MLSS—
Overflow rateiSurface overflow rate (SOR). Upward water velocity in the clarifier. High flow raises it and floats solids over the weir.<33 m/d avg · >40 fails—
LoadingiSolids loading vs capacity. Solids flux onto the clarifier relative to what it can handle at the current SVI. Over 100% = blanket rises.Keep <90%—

Run the Plant — Activated Sludge

You are the operator on shift at a 20,000 m³/d (≈5.3 MGD) conventional activated sludge plant. Keep the bugs healthy, the sludge settling, and the effluent inside your NPDES permit — for 30 days, through everything the weather and the sewer throw at you.

Your permit — don't exceed
BOD ≤ 30 mg/LTSS ≤ 30 mg/LAmmonia ≤ 3 mg/L
Your four levers
WASWasting rate. The master control. Waste more to drop MLSS and sludge age; waste less to build them. This is how you set SRT.
RASReturn activated sludge. Pumps settled solids from the clarifier back to the basin. Raise it to pull down a rising blanket in wet weather.
DODissolved oxygen setpoint. Keep it near 2 mg/L: too low stalls nitrification and breeds filaments; too high burns energy.
Cl₂Emergency RAS chlorination. Knocks back a filament bulking event fast — but it costs money and is a blunt instrument.

Set your controls, hit Advance 1 Day, and read the board. Watch the Operator's Log — it will coach you when something drifts. Score = days in spec, at the lowest cost.
