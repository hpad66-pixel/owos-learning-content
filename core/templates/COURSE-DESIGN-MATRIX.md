## Course-level review

- Are adjacent modules structurally distinct?
- Does visual variety follow the concepts rather than decoration?
- Does every listed visual manifest ID resolve to an actual asset or registered executable component?
- Are simulations used for processes, algorithms, consequences, and changing systems?
- Does each module create a different but relevant professional action?
- Are quizzes varied and matched to the type of knowledge?
- Does every module preserve accessibility, mobile behavior, and deterministic feedback?
- Do repeated components earn their repetition because the same cognitive job recurs?
- Does the course meet the minimum archetype count in `COURSE-EXPERIENCE-ARCHITECTURE.md`?
- Does the rendered course pass `tools/course_distinctiveness.py`?
- Do all modules have approved storyboards and rendered desktop, tablet, and phone evidence?
- Does the completed course coherence report show that terminology, scenarios, work products, and
  assessments form one deliberate sequence?
