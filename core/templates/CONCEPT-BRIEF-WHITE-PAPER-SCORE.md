### Scoring rules

- Award only points supported by the current saved white paper and its research record.
- Do not award research or verification points for an unsupported outline.
- Explain every deduction.
- List the smallest useful next actions that would raise the score.
- A score of 90 to 100 is eligible for owner approval into curriculum design.
- A score of 80 to 89 is strong but requires revision.
- A score of 70 to 79 has material teaching or evidence gaps.
- A score below 70 returns to sparring and research.
- No score overrides a blocked hard gate.
