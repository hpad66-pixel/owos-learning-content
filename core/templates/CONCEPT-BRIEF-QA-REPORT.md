## Learning capability and continuing-education checks

- [ ] Every capability identifier resolves through `core/learning-capabilities/registry.yaml`.
- [ ] At least two substantial explanatory visuals are real and reviewed.
- [ ] The dynamic mechanism teaches change, cause, consequence, sequence, dependency, or hidden
  structure.
- [ ] Keyboard, touch, mobile, reduced-motion, no-JavaScript, and structured-text meanings agree.
- [ ] At least two checks are distributed where the concept is taught.
- [ ] Immediate explanatory feedback and retry are present.
- [ ] A final applied check demonstrates transfer.
- [ ] Completion evidence is deterministic and versioned.
- [ ] Placement, prerequisites, remediation, and next-learning relationships are explicit.
- [ ] Every used capability is locked to the authoritative registry version.
- [ ] Learning-event identifiers are stable; consent, record authority, privacy, and retention are
  explicit; facility-sensitive data collection is prohibited.
- [ ] Prior completion versions are preserved and material corrections have a notification and
  supersession path.
- [ ] Assessment passing, retry, item-version, feedback, and accommodation policies are explicit.
- [ ] Every dynamic model has an identifier, version, visible assumptions, deterministic replay,
  qualified review, and a prohibition on operational use.
- [ ] Language, units, localization, and instructional-time measurement are explicit.
- [ ] Cross-sector transfer preserves different source, process, procedure, and authority boundaries.
- [ ] Continuing-education credit claims remain disabled unless the named accreditor approved the
  exact offering.

## Learner-economy checks

- [ ] A short “In 30 seconds” orientation appears near the beginning.
- [ ] Primary in-page navigation contains no more than four controls.
- [ ] Claim counts, verification inventories, and raw Graph or workflow metadata are absent from
  learner-facing promotional copy.
- [ ] The public SOP value stops at an outline or checklist; facility-specific generation is kept in
  a separately governed authenticated agent.
- [ ] The complete Community interface is not duplicated inside the brief.
- [ ] One compact comment form appears after teaching and active commercial content, immediately
  before the footer.
- [ ] The closing recap contains exactly “What changed,” “What to observe,” and “What not to
  assume.”
- [ ] Inactive vendor placements are hidden and active placements retain the editorial-independence
  disclosure.
- [ ] The short facility-judgment disclaimer matches the approved plain-language form.
- [ ] Positive feedback requires explicit testimonial consent and separate moderator approval before
  public display.
- [ ] Featured testimonials expose only consented identity fields and are labeled as learner
  experience, not technical evidence or vendor endorsement.
- [ ] The administrator can inspect views, unique viewers, engaged reads, completion reaches,
  comments, reviewed comments, consented appreciation, and featured testimonials.
