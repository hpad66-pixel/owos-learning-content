# Master OWOS Concept Brief Activation Prompt

This is the canonical activation prompt for every portfolio candidate. Replace the bracketed fields
with the activation seed from one candidate record. Exact mirrors in inventory repositories are
validated against this file.

```text
Activate the governed OWOS Concept Engine for the Concept Brief titled “[TITLE].”

Treat this portfolio record as Hardeep's initial product direction, not as verified evidence:

[ACTIVATION SEED]

Begin in sparring and intake. First sharpen the learner job, practical consequence, concept
boundary, misconceptions, adjacent concepts, and the question the brief must help a utility
professional ask. Preserve my direction and clearly identify anything that is forced, incomplete,
unsafe, redundant, or better suited to a Dictionary entry, course, facility procedure, private
tenant brief, or jurisdiction-specific work product.

Run the complete OWOS Concept Engine lifecycle under owos-concept-brief/2. Build the material-claim
register before public prose. Public water, wastewater, stormwater, and One Water Concept Briefs use
current United States federal primary authority and EPA guidance. Exclude state requirements from
the public authority frame, claims, citations, comparisons, Graph evidence, and learner narrative.
When state or permit applicability matters, state only that jurisdiction-specific review is needed
and route the detail to a separately governed private work product. Exclude non-United States
regulations, standards, government guidance, design guides, operator guides, and health guidelines
from governing evidence. Verify original sources, preserve exact locators, expose uncertainty, and
require independent and qualified review.

Develop an evidence-backed narrative, a unique Graphite design fingerprint, and a beat-by-beat
storyboard. Do not copy the Coagulation vs Flocculation page composition. Select visuals,
interactions, role treatments, Graph relationships, Community questions, and a closing action from
this concept's actual learning job. Every visual and interaction must teach something, work on
mobile, remain accessible, and have reduced-motion, no-JavaScript, and structured-text equivalents.

Resolve visual, interactive, animated, and assessment capabilities from the authoritative
owos-learning-content core/learning-capabilities/registry.yaml. Do not duplicate or fork shared
component definitions in this portfolio or the Concept Brief package. Treat the brief as one
focused learning module with at least two substantial explanatory visuals, one dynamic concept
mechanism, two distributed checks, one final applied transfer check, deterministic completion
evidence, and a cross-sector connection unless an approved exception is more accurate.

Complete the durable learning-system records before implementation: placement, prerequisites,
remediation and next learning; the exact shared-registry version and every used capability
identifier; stable event names, attempt history, consent, record authority, privacy and retention;
completion-version preservation, supersession and material-correction notification; assessment
passing, retry, item-version, feedback and accommodation policy; dynamic-model identity, version,
assumptions, deterministic replay, qualified review and prohibition on operational use; and
language, units, localization and measured instructional-time method. Do not collect
facility-sensitive data in a public Concept Brief.

Apply the learner-economy contract: a short orientation, four or fewer primary controls, no public
claim-count marketing, one compact end feedback entry, the complete Community experience in its
drawer or route, outline-only public SOP value, hidden inactive commercial placements, and the
approved three-part closing recap and short disclaimer.

Create credential-readiness and learning-path metadata without issuing a credential. Map exact
completion evidence to the shared OWOS learning-record contract, xAPI event profile, optional
SCORM 2004 adapter, cmi5 launch profile, competency and cross-sector Graph nodes, and future Open
Badges 3.0 or Comprehensive Learner Record export. Keep every CEU, PDH, CU, contact-hour,
certificate, badge, and accreditor claim disabled until authenticated learner identity, exact
offering approval, measured time, passing evidence, provider approval, and the named accreditor or
jurisdiction requirements are verified.

Define explainable next-learning candidates in three lanes: deepen the learner's current role,
reskill toward a new role or competency, and cross-skill into adjacent drinking water, wastewater,
stormwater, One Water, data, leadership, or artificial-intelligence topics. Recommendations are
advisory and consent-based. They must show why the suggestion was made, what evidence was used,
what prerequisite is missing, and how the learner can dismiss or correct it. Do not infer protected
traits, employee performance, or facility conditions.

Pause for my approval of the evidence boundary, narrative direction, design fingerprint, and
storyboard before production implementation. After approval, create the governed structured
package, compile the working preview, run portfolio distinctiveness and all automated checks, and
report every unresolved factual, practitioner, accessibility, Graph, Community, commercial,
credential, privacy, and release gate. Never describe a preview as released or publish without my
explicit approval.
```
