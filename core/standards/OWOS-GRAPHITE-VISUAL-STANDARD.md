# OWOS Graphite Visual Standard

Graphite is the default visual identity for new public OWOS knowledge and learning products,
including Concept Briefs, courses, articles, reports, public SOP templates, learning-document
previews, and their landing pages.

Graphite locks the brand language. It does not impose one page layout. Every product still selects
its narrative structure, visuals, interactions, information density, and composition from the
reader's job and the approved product design brief.

## Core tokens

| Raised surface | `--owos-charcoal` | `#292826` |

## Color discipline

- Graphite and charcoal carry the main experience.
- Water blue identifies the concept, navigation, focus, active state, and explanatory flow.
- Amber is reserved for caution, cost, consequence, and the one point that deserves emphasis.
- Green is reserved for a favorable, verified, live, or stable state.
- Red is reserved for critical, failed, unsafe, or blocked states.
- Do not use accent colors as decoration.
- A pale surface is an approved exception for print, long-form accessibility, or a dense evidence
  view only when the design brief explains why it improves reading.

## Typography

- Primary display and body stack: `Arial, Barlow, sans-serif`.
- Technical, source, data, and navigation stack: `"Courier New", "Space Mono", monospace`.
- Editorial serif is optional and limited to a quotation or intentionally distinct long-form voice.
- Headings are direct, heavy, and compact. Do not force uppercase everywhere.
- Technical labels use small mono type with generous letter spacing.
- Body copy remains readable, normally at least 16 pixels with a line height near 1.6.

## Composition

- Lead with one illuminated idea, not a dashboard of equal cards.
- Use generous breathing room and calm alignment.
- Use subtle depth, thin borders, restrained glow, and limited shadow.
- Numbered sections must remain clearly readable. Faded decoration cannot carry required meaning.
- Ambient typography may support atmosphere only when it is fully legible or clearly decorative and
  does not collide with headings or controls.
- Keep controls purposeful. A control must change, test, compare, reveal, or produce something.
- Preserve varied silhouettes across adjacent lessons and briefs. Brand consistency is not a reason
  to repeat one composition.

## Visual and motion behavior

- Graphics must teach a relationship, process, cause, change, comparison, quantity, or consequence.
- Motion must reveal meaning and provide an equivalent reduced-motion state.
- The Graphite palette does not make a decorative card, icon row, glow, or animation instructional.
- Every visual still needs its reading guide, learner conclusion, accessible equivalent, mobile
  transformation, provenance, and rendered review.

## Accessibility

- Primary text on graphite and charcoal surfaces uses `#F2F1EC` or another tested light color.
- Muted text must still pass the required contrast for its size and role.
- Water blue, amber, green, and red cannot be the only carrier of meaning.
- Focus indicators must be visible on every surface.
- Verify desktop, tablet, phone, keyboard, touch, screen reader, reduced motion, 200 percent reflow,
  and 400 percent reflow before release.

## Scope and migration

- New public knowledge and learning products use Graphite by default.
- Existing public knowledge products adopt Graphite when materially revised.
- Existing operational application and administration screens may remain on the Clearwater system
  until their product owner approves migration.
- A product may depart from Graphite only through a recorded design exception approved by Hardeep.
- A visual approval never approves claims, technical accuracy, Graph publication, Community
  connection, commercial placement, or release.
