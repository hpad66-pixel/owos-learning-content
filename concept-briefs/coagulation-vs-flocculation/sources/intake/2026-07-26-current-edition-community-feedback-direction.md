# Current edition and Community feedback direction

Hardeep directed that the public Concept Brief must not be presented as a candidate. It should be
available as the current educational edition, supported by the quality-control work already
performed.

Readers should be able to:

- enter the Community from the top of the brief;
- use the complete Community section;
- leave a compact blog-style comment at the bottom;
- submit technical corrections, source suggestions, questions, or field observations; and
- understand that feedback is welcome and may lead to a governed revision.

All feedback must reach the administrator dashboard. The administrator must be able to respond,
mark feedback reviewed or incorporated, revise the structured content, and retain version history.
Feedback never silently overwrites the current edition.
