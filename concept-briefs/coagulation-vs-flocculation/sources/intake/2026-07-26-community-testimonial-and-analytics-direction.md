# Community testimonial and administrator analytics direction

Hardeep approved an extension of the existing Concept Brief Community workflow so learners can share
what helped them, including value they found in cross-sector learning.

The approved product requirements are:

- add a positive-feedback type to the existing end-of-brief comment form;
- let a learner optionally grant permission for OWOS to publish that exact comment with the
  learner's name, role, and organization;
- require separate OWOS moderator approval before a consented comment appears publicly;
- let the administrator remove a featured comment without deleting its Community and moderation
  history;
- render approved comments as a polished, topic-specific reader-voices section near the bottom of
  the brief;
- label testimonials as learner experience rather than technical evidence, verified instruction,
  or vendor endorsement; and
- give the super administrator topic-level views, recent unique viewers, engaged reads, completion
  reaches, comments, reviewed comments, consented positive feedback, and featured-testimonial counts.

The existing Community, Concept Brief Author Studio, version-control, moderation, privacy, and
editorial-independence systems remain authoritative. This direction does not authorize automatic
publication, invented testimonials, or reuse beyond the consented Concept Brief.
