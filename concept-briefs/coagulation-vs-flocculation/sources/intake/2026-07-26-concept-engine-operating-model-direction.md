# Concept Engine operating-model direction

Owner: Hardeep Anand  
Captured: 2026-07-26  
Visibility: internal product direction

Hardeep directed the complete pilot workflow to become the repeatable Concept Engine operating
model. The lifecycle must preserve sparring, analysis, approved Perplexity research, original-source
review, a second OpenAI or independent reverse QA/QC pass across the complete claim list, claim
correction, scoring, visual production, vendor and APAS placement, Graph, Community, final QA, and
publication control.

Concept Briefs must not create a confusing second learning destination. They should live in the same
OWOS Learn experience as Master Classes while remaining a visibly different content type.

The backend must give APAS complete control over:

- governed content and version updates;
- research and claim-review state;
- preview, approval, publication, supersession, rollback, and correction;
- Graph nodes and relationships;
- Community context, moderation, verified answers, and correction escalation;
- APAS and vendor logos, copy, links, targeting, dates, pause, removal, and directory connection;
- vendor-facing aggregate engagement reporting; and
- the editorial firewall that prevents commercial participation from influencing evidence.

This direction authorizes specification and implementation planning. It does not waive technical,
accessibility, security, legal, commercial-conflict, Graph, Community, or release gates.
