# Guided decision rehearsal direction

Hardeep Anand directed the Concept Brief to replace the cryptic applied-transfer instruction with a
learner-visible simulation.

The experience must:

- show a realistic example before asking the learner to respond;
- demonstrate how a tempting shortcut becomes a responsible evidence question;
- offer a nearby “Try it yourself” action;
- state clearly that personal practice is optional;
- avoid collecting facility-sensitive information or presenting an operating recommendation; and
- use the same governed learning capability approach that supports OWOS courses.

Hardeep also asked whether the brief is connected to courses. The implementation must distinguish
shared engine capability from an actual course-route connection and must not invent a course that
does not exist.
