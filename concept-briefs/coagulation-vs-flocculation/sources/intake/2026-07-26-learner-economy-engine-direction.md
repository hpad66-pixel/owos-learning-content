# Learner economy and Concept Engine implementation direction

Hardeep approved implementation of the learner-facing recommendations across the pilot, the OWOS
Concept Brief Compiler, the Concept Engine production contract, templates, tests, platform
connections, and GitHub repository documentation.

The approved requirements are:

- add a concise “In 30 seconds” orientation near the top;
- limit primary navigation to Start, Live Jar, Sources, and Community;
- remove the governed-claim count from learner-facing catalog copy;
- keep SOP value to an outline and reserve facility-specific generation for a separate authenticated
  agent;
- provide one compact comment form at the true end and use the existing Community drawer or route
  for the complete discussion experience;
- place APAS and active commercial connections after the teaching;
- hide inactive vendor placeholders;
- retain the commercial editorial-independence disclosure and administrator control;
- end with “What changed,” “What to observe,” and “What not to assume”; and
- use the short disclaimer: “This brief explains the concept. Facility decisions still require your
  approved procedures and qualified judgment.”

These requirements apply to the reusable engine and future Concept Briefs. They must not be
implemented only as one-off edits to the pilot HTML.
