# Universal connected-learning drawers

Captured: 2026-07-26

Hardeep Anand directed that every course and Concept Brief show Graph and Community controls at the
top. Both must open as temporary right-side drawers over the current HTML page. When a learner
closes the drawer, presses Escape, selects the backdrop, or uses browser Back, the drawer must
vanish and the unchanged learning page must remain.

The implementation belongs in the Course Engine, Concept Engine, shared platform runtime, templates,
tests, and rendered QA. It must not remain a manual correction to one publication.

This is product and learning-experience direction. It is not factual, technical, regulatory, Graph
publication, Community-content, credential, commercial, or release evidence.
