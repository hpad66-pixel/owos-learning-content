# Detention, Retention, and Infiltration

**Domain:** Stormwater  
**Portfolio job:** Connect practice names to actual water pathways.

## Value thesis

Stormwater practices store, release, retain, evaporate, infiltrate, or treat runoff in different
ways. This brief should follow one storm through each relevant mechanism and expose effects on peak
flow, runoff volume, pollutants, groundwater interaction, and maintenance. Its value is preventing a
practice label from creating expectations the asset was not designed to meet.

## Intended outcome

The learner can describe the water pathway and performance objective before naming or evaluating the
practice.

## Activation seed

Build a Concept Brief that routes the same storm through detention, retention, and infiltration
systems. Research terminology variation and avoid presenting regional naming conventions as
universal. Center the experience on water pathways, stated objectives, site constraints, and
maintenance evidence.
