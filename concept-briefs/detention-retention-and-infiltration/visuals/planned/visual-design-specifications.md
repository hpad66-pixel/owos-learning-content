# Proposed visual design specifications

These are design-stage specifications, not production graphics. They allow each proposed visual to
remain separately identified and reviewable before owner authorization for implementation.

## Connected storm route

A panoramic system cutaway from rainfall through surface routes, dry and wet storage, subsurface
movement, outlet, overflow, receiving water, and unintended sanitary-sewer entry.

## Dry-basin timeline

Five aligned states: before, rising, peak, recession, and recovery. Inflow, water level, storage,
outlet, and overflow remain in the same positions across states.

## Wet-pond elevations

A vertical cutaway separating pond bottom, permanent pool, normal water level, temporary storm
storage, outlet, overflow, and downstream water level.

## Practice-family cutaways

Three structurally aligned sections for bioretention, infiltration trench, and permeable pavement.
Each shows surface entry, storage, media, native soil, underdrain, groundwater, and overflow.

## Qualitative hydrograph

Paired inflow and outflow curves with authored before, rise, peak, recession, and recovery
annotations. No transferable numeric axis or model result.

## Water and pollutant ledger

A quantity-flow explanation that reads water volume first, concentration second, and total
pollutant load third.

## Maintenance failure trace

Condition, pathway question, possible consequence, and next evidence remain visibly separate.

## Stormwater and wastewater split

One rainfall header above the intended stormwater route and unintended infiltration and inflow
routes into a separate sanitary sewer.

## Permit-to-value handoff

Public objective, approved condition, responsible party, access and funded work, evidence, and
correction appear as an accountable sequence.

## Five ledgers

One storm connects to water, environmental, asset, financial, and public-accountability questions.

## Role evidence map

Engineering, operations, finance, leadership, wastewater, and public questions attach to one shared
route, condition, responsibility, and evidence record.
