# Owner Format and Instruction Revision, Round 10

Date: 2026-07-26

## Owner assessment

The owner rejected preview 0.2 as weak overall. The specific findings were:

- the left margin and editorial gutters were not acceptable;
- graphic text needed alignment checks;
- instruction was weak;
- the topics were not introduced before the learner was asked to use them; and
- definitions were not established early enough.

## Revision decision

Preview 0.3 returns the rendered experience to owner review. It does not change the approved
white-paper research direction and does not authorize release.

The revision:

- adds a thirteenth foundation beat before the storm tracer;
- defines detention, retention, infiltration, stormwater runoff, storage, outlet, outfall,
  overflow, bypass, permanent pool, and underdrain in visible plain English;
- introduces dry detention, wet ponds, subsurface practices, hydrographs, performance measures,
  maintenance consequences, wastewater infiltration and inflow, permits, financial value, and role
  questions before their visuals;
- increases desktop and laptop editorial gutters;
- limits instructor prose to a controlled reading measure;
- frames and aligns explanatory graphics and captions consistently;
- converts the foundation definition table into stacked labeled records on phones; and
- expands rendered browser evidence to include the foundation and individual visual blocks.

## Open gates

Owner review of preview 0.3 is pending. Independent verification, qualified practitioner review,
accessibility review, novice-reader review, and release approval remain open.
