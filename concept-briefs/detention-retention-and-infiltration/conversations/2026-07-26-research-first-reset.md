# Research-first reset direction

Date: 2026-07-26

Owner: Hardeep Anand

Status: Active direction

## What was rejected

The first working preview moved into HTML production before the teaching argument and research were
complete. Its margins, prose measure, and editorial formatting did not meet the quality established
by the Coagulation versus Flocculation reference.

The preview is frozen as a pre-research prototype. It is not the curriculum basis and is not
approved for release.

## New governing direction

For this and future Concept Briefs, begin with questions and owner sparring. Establish what is being
taught, why it is being taught, why it matters, who needs it, what misconceptions must be repaired,
how deep the treatment should go, and what belongs outside scope.

The first generated teaching artifact is a clean Markdown white paper. It must be a complete,
beautifully structured explanation with headings, subheadings, plain-language teaching, research,
evidence, diagrams and graphic briefs, limitations, curriculum implications, references, quality
control, and a score.

Revise that Markdown file through repeated owner sparring. Only after the owner approves its
teaching thesis and direction may the Concept Engine proceed into claim verification, design,
storyboarding, graphics, interactions, and HTML.
