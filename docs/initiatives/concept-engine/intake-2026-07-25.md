## Hardeep direction captured

- Create a distinct visual identity for concept experiences using black, white, blue, and gold.
- Break up an all-black page with alternating light and dark surfaces.
- Use blue primarily for section-heading structure.
- Keep teaching animations on black where the mechanism is easiest to see.
- Determine the value for operators, designers, engineers, consultants, utility executives, vendors,
  and other participants.
- Determine what additional value would make a one-page concept experience useful.
- Explore visible, linked participation for vendors, engineers, consultants, and other organizations.
- Make the commercial value explicit without compromising trust.
- Connect the result to the OWOS knowledge graph.
- Decide where Concept Briefs, lexicon terms, courses, use cases, and role experiences sit in the
  navigation.
- Avoid clutter and confusion.
- Determine whether the Concept Engine should become a reusable Codex skill.

## Disposition

The initiative evaluation is recorded in `docs/initiatives/concept-engine/README.md`.

On 2026-07-25, Hardeep approved the recommendations, especially the private standard-operating-
procedure and tenant value, and directed OWOS to build the complete Concept Brief lifecycle. The
direction requires:

- accepting diagrams, concepts, articles, briefs, procedures, standard operating procedures, and
  other supplied material;
- preserving and researching the source;
- designing each brief separately and uniquely;
- compiling through a versioned Concept Brief system;
- requiring complete claim-verification coverage and qualified technical review;
- connecting the Graph and the existing Community forum;
- preserving the commercial and editorial sensitivities already recorded; and
- building the system before treating the pilot as released content.

The approved implementation creates a versioned compiler and a repository skill. The skill does not
mass-produce fixed nine-section pages and cannot approve its own facts.

## Permanent United States authority direction

On 2026-07-25, Hardeep directed that OWOS water-sector content use United States standards and
authorities only. The Concept Engine must:

- begin with current United States federal primary authority;
- add state requirements only when the state, applicability, and effective date are explicit;
- exclude non-United States regulations, standards, government guidance, design guides, operator
  guides, and health guidelines from the source register, claim basis, narrative, Graph, Community
  verification, and reviewer dossier; and
- treat research conducted outside the United States only as bounded research, never as a governing
  standard.

This is a durable repository, skill, contract, compiler, and QA rule rather than a one-brief editing
preference.
